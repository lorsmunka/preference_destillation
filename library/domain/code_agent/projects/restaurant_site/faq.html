Questions about booking

Can I book for 12 people?
Yes, in one of our private rooms.
Do I pay a deposit?
Only for tables of 6 or more, $10 per guest.
What if I am late?
Call us. After 15 minutes we may give the table away.

Food and allergies

Can you cook gluten free?
Most dishes, if you tell us when you book.
Is there a vegan menu?
Every dish marked (vg) is vegan.
Is there a children's menu?
Yes, two courses for $9 until 19:00.
