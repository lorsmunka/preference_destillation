Code of conduct

CodeCoast is for everyone. We do not accept harassment.

  • Be kind and respectful, in talks and online.
  • No offensive images or jokes in any talk.
  • Ask before you take photos of people.

Reporting a problem

Talk to any organiser in a red T-shirt.

Or call the safety line on 555 0188, open all three days.

What happens next

Organisers may warn a person or send them away without a refund.
