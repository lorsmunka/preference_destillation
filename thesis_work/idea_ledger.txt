IDEA LEDGER — distillation thesis / novelty search
What was tried, and (1 sentence) why it got shot down. Last updated 2026-08-26.
Verdict key:  SCOOPED = owned by prior work · WOUNDED = mechanism owned, only a
narrow setting survives · MEASURED = killed by our own experiment · SURVIVES =
still standing (thin).
========================================================================

LOSS / SCHEDULE FAMILY
----------------------
1. Loss-schedule sweep (KL/CE mixing ratios, temp annealing, KL->CE vs CE->KL)
   SCOOPED — the mechanism is Hinton/Annealing-KD/CTKD/DKD, and the CE~=KL
   outcome is already predicted by Menon (ICML 2021).

2. Sharpness crossover ("KL marginal on sharp domains, decisive on free-form")
   SCOOPED — near-identical concurrent ICML 2026 work: Bridge-Garden
   (2605.26246) + EGAD (2605.01732), entropy-keyed, verified real.

3. CE->KL reverse-direction annealing as "the untested direction"
   WOUNDED/trivial — "anneal toward the distribution you want at the end" is
   close to a tautology, and the mixing-wins result is Bridge-Garden's.

VOCABULARY FAMILY
-----------------
4. Output-vocab floor / collapse curve (how small before KL dies)
   WOUNDED — Kolesnikova (2205.02340) already plots KD-quality vs vocab size
   (and found no clean collapse); Subclass Distillation owns "few classes
   starve dark knowledge."

5. Best auxiliary-token selection (mass-ranked aux tokens)
   SCOOPED — token selection by teacher mass is Sparse Logit Sampling
   (2503.16870) / Selective KD (2602.01395) / top-K logit distillation.

6. Retained-mass predictor (cheap pass predicts where KL collapses under
   vocab truncation)
   MEASURED (no-go) — on our own machine both sharp and soft domains retain
   ~99% at their operating vocab; entropy (0.02 vs 0.79) is the real
   discriminator, so retained mass adds nothing; metric also already named
   ("Don't Ignore the Tail", 2602.20816, ICML 2026).

PROMPT / CONTEXT FAMILY
-----------------------
7. Prompt internalization into a tiny from-scratch student
   WOUNDED — the mechanism is Snell 2022 "Learning by Distilling Context"
   verbatim (SKIntern, PromptIntern, Gist Tokens pile on); only the tiny scale
   is new.

8. Co-compression interaction (does internalization survive when the vocab is
   ALSO starved to ~500, from scratch?)
   SURVIVES (thin) — the joint corner is genuinely unmeasured, but it's an
   existence-proof / combination, not a new mechanism.

SYSTEMS / DEPLOYMENT FAMILY
---------------------------
9. Tool-call tutor (tiny expert emits calculator/tool calls; math tutor)
   SCOOPED — every verb is owned: Toolformer, Calc-X/Calcformers, PAL/PoT,
   Agent Distillation (2505.17612).

10. Chain of tiny distilled experts vs MoE on edge
    SCOOPED + PREMISE REFUTED — NVIDIA 2506.02153 + Mixture-of-Modular-Experts
    2407.19610 own the comparison, and 2606.21428 shows edge is memory-bound,
    not compute-bound (killing the whole "FLOPS is the constraint" framing).

INTERPRETABILITY / SCALE FAMILY
-------------------------------
11. CE-vs-KL representation comparison ("same accuracy, same model inside?")
    SCOOPED — Muller 2019 (soft changes geometry at equal accuracy) + Stanton
    2021 (accuracy != internal fidelity) + Distilled Circuits (2505.10822,
    generative, 2025); only the accuracy-matched seed-baseline hygiene + tiny
    sandbox survive.

12. Calibration transfer into tiny reduced-vocab students (+ deferral cascade)
    SCOOPED hard — Calibration-transfer-via-KD (2302.11472), teacher-calibration
    (2508.20224), Gatekeeper cascade (2502.19335), Muller 2019; the closed-vocab
    "clean ECE" pitch is backwards (classification is the cleanest setting).

13. Reasoning scale floor (min size/vocab where reasoning distills)
    SCOOPED — SCoTD (2306.14050), and Teaching Arithmetic to Small Transformers
    (2307.03381) already does sub-10M FROM-SCRATCH verifiable multi-step
    arithmetic; sub-10M is likely below the floor, so the curve is flat-at-chance.

STORAGE / PRECISION FAMILY
--------------------------
16. Mass-graded teacher-logit storage precision (more bits to high-mass tokens,
    fewer to the tail; store full distribution at graded bit-depth)
    SCOOPED/TRIVIAL — it's the KL-optimal solution, proven (ISIT 2021/2022
    companders x^2/3 / ArcSinh) and shipped (Arcee DistillKit 2024); only a
    "benchmark the KL-optimal compander on LLM logits" engineering sliver
    survives, AND the benefit is suspect (Don't Ignore the Tail 2602.20816 says
    the tail carries the signal you'd be degrading).

17. Progressive output-vocab REDUCTION during training (wide->narrow curriculum)
    SCOOPED — DeepKD (2505.15133, Dynamic Top-K Masking) and PCD (2505.24310,
    C2FL branch) already schedule the KD output-support SIZE during training in
    both directions (May 2025); "not annealing" is true but doesn't help since
    the size-schedule axis itself is now claimed; only a reduced-vocab-AR
    head-physically-shrinks engineering delta survives.

METHOD / ARCHITECTURE FAMILY (big swings)
-----------------------------------------
18. Learned/differentiable output vocabulary (learn which tokens to keep, vs
    mass-ranking)
    SCOOPED + BENEFIT-NULL — VVD (NAACL 2019) owns learned-selection-beats-
    frequency (on input embeddings); porting to the KD output head is a thin
    "old technique, new layer," and theory says the gain over mass-ranking is
    ~zero because the KL loss is already mass-weighted.

19. Shared tiny body + swappable per-domain reduced-vocab heads (modular
    on-device, one body many domains)
    SCOOPED + VALUE-PROP KILLED — classic MTL shared-trunk/heads (MT-DNN,
    HydraNet, Apple-2024, MT-DNN+KD 1904.09482); only reduced-vocab-head-as-unit
    at sub-10M is unclaimed, and negative transfer at tiny scale (Stem Cell
    Hypothesis, EMNLP 2021) likely makes independent experts beat the shared
    body, killing the memory-savings claim. Base task itself near-scooped by
    2607.08268 (2026, sub-1B on-device closed-vocab sentiment/tone JSON).

SAFETY / LIFECYCLE / EXTRACTION FAMILY (big swings, batch 2)
-----------------------------------------------------------
20. Reduced vocab as a HARD safety guarantee (can't emit harmful tokens)
    DEAD — it's a manual token filter with extra steps, bypassable by coded
    language (like humans); "can't say slurs because I filter 'shit'" is a
    regex, not a thesis.

21. Continual output-vocab expansion for a deployed tiny distilled expert under
    drift (grow vocab online, no forgetting)
    SCOOPED (mechanism) — Markov-Matrix zero-forgetting expansion (2605.04308),
    ASR continual output-vocab expansion (2401.04482), class-incremental
    distillation all own it; surviving sliver = the capacity-vs-forgetting
    frontier at sub-10M where freeze-backbone zero-forgetting BREAKS (tiny model
    must move shared weights to absorb new tokens) — a real "why scale matters"
    reason, but a characterization, not a method.

22. Reverse-compile a tiny distilled net into explicit code/rules
    WEAK FOR OUR DOMAINS — sentiment extraction yields a trivial keyword
    classifier (undercuts the generative premise); math extraction yields a
    BROKEN heuristic (our model gets arithmetic wrong ~22%); only pays off on a
    task with a clean hidden algorithm, which ours aren't.

TEACHER-SIDE / DATA-GENERATION FAMILY (batch 3)
-----------------------------------------------
23. Teacher reasons at length, tiny student emits ONLY the answer (does
    teacher-side reasoning help a non-reasoning student; capacity floor)
    OWNED (headline) — Wadhwa "Mysteries of CoT-Augmented Distillation"
    (2406.14511, EMNLP 2024) does it on small models incl. the "how much
    reasoning" answer (>60% CoT-mask before collapse); Dual-Head (2509.21487);
    capacity floor (2502.18001, minimum-width 2604.04037).
    *** SLIVER WORTH KEEPING *** — Wadhwa/Dual-Head put reasoning tokens IN the
    student target; ours would transfer reasoning PURELY via the answer's soft
    distribution (logit-KL over ~525 vocab), ZERO reasoning tokens in the
    student. "Does reasoning transfer through soft labels alone?" is a clean
    falsifiable mechanistic question nobody isolates; benefit uncertain (Wadhwa
    implies it may NOT), but a negative result is still a real finding.

24. Teacher-prompt as dominant student lever + internalization pitfalls
    SCOOPED — Snell 2022 (2209.15189: exact setup + capacity ceiling); the
    pitfalls paper already exists (GenPI 2411.15927, NAACL 2025); teacher-prompt-
    as-lever = Distilling Step-by-Step / SCoTD; prompt-vs-generator decomposition
    = 2604.13977. Sliver: prompt-vs-recipe attribution in the reduced-vocab
    internalization regime — a measurement only. NOT shelved (pitfalls scooped).

25. Confidence/entropy-curated distillation data to cut generation cost
    SCOOPED — LLM on a Budget (2511.11574, up to 80% fewer teacher labels);
    Distill on a Diet (2606.25488); sample selection by teacher signal
    (2404.03693). Sliver: reduced-vocab generative measurement only. NOT shelved.

26. Distill Gemma (transformer) into a JEPA student
    SCOOPED + PREMISE-CONFLICT — "KD via JEPA latent prediction" is owned
    title-level (JEP-KD 2403.18843) on top of the ancient feature-KD genre
    (FitNets/TinyBERT); "JEPA x language" is owned by the LeCun orbit itself
    (LLM-JEPA 2509.14252; VL-JEPA 2512.10942 = predicts text EMBEDDINGS instead
    of AR tokens, 1.6B, LeCun+Fung; JEPA-Reasoner 2512.19171; LLM2Vec-Gen
    2603.10913 mines LLM-teacher->latent-student in 2026). Deeper: JEPA is an
    OBJECTIVE around a transformer, not an alternative architecture, and a JEPA
    student has NO softmax head — deleting the reduced-vocab/logit-KL axis (the
    ANCHOR) and turning the JSON task into the classifier the thesis exists to
    avoid. Scale floor: text-JEPA is 1.6B+ frontier-lab territory. Sliver:
    JEPA-style latent aux-loss beside logit-KL at the tiny floor = C1's
    "feature distillation unexplored" = same replication species as S1-S4.

27. Distill via speculative decoding (student drafts training data, teacher
    verifies/replaces tokens)
    SCOOPED at title level — the exact mechanism is SKD (2410.11325, Google,
    ICLR 2025): student drafts, teacher top-K accept/replace, interleaved
    sequence = training data. Surrounding space carpet-bombed: principle =
    GKD 2306.13649 / MiniLLM / ImitKD; reverse direction = DistillSpec
    2310.08461; loss-level accept/reject = SpecKD 2510.24021; deployment-time
    = Online Spec Decoding 2310.07177; 2026 niche-of-niche = Draft-OPD
    2605.29343 + H-OPD 2607.02592; on-policy KD = standard recipe since
    Thinking Machines blog (2025). ALSO wrong for our pipeline: puts teacher
    in the training loop (kills the offline saved-full-distribution asset,
    infeasible on Mac) and its motivation (exposure bias in long free-form
    gen) ~vanishes for short deterministic JSON. Sliver (weakest yet, do NOT
    board): nobody runs on-policy KD on a FROM-SCRATCH student (early drafts
    = noise -> acceptance ~0 -> degenerates to supervised KD); "when does
    on-policy start mattering from scratch" = replication-at-floor + costly.

28. Watcher model predicts student correctness from its attention patterns
    SCOOPED (saturated 2024-2026) — attention-map correctness/hallucination
    classifiers: Lookback Lens (2407.07071), spectral attention features
    (2502.17598), attention-head entropy predicts correctness; hidden-state
    probes: question-only linear probes predict accuracy BEFORE generation
    (2509.10625), self-failure via internal circuits incl. attention
    (2512.20578), look-ahead correctness in states (2506.23274), entropy-trace
    monitoring (2601.09001); the deferral USE CASE already died as #12
    (Gatekeeper). At 3L/525-vocab, plain output entropy likely predicts errors
    anyway (cheaper than any probe). Sliver: probe-as-INSTRUMENT for S6 error
    analysis (attention encodes the failure mode), not a paper.

29. Answer-slot auxiliary supervision (reserve part of output/hidden state to
    hold the fixed-length final answer during training, aux loss on it)
    SCOOPED (family) — answer-head beside reasoning-head = Dual-Head KD
    (2509.21487, already owned #23); aux future/target prediction = MTP
    (2404.19737, F5: helps big / HURTS small); latent-target aux losses =
    Hierarchical Latent Prediction (2608.05806) + activation-space process
    supervision + Coconut (2412.06769); deep supervision is 2015; Allen-Zhu
    Physics 2.1 owns the phenomenon (answer precomputed in hidden states).
    Sliver: the INTERVENTION at the tiny distilled floor as ONE audit row
    (math templates give free ground truth; F5's conflict predicts a possible
    clean negative at 3.4M via capacity competition). Not a paper.

REGULARIZATION / OPTIMIZER / MoE FAMILY (batch 4, 2026-08-26)
------------------------------------------------------------
30. Gaussian noise on attention patterns / embeddings to delay overfitting in
    tiny distilled students ("sees more examples before it overfits")
    WOUNDED — every injection point named is owned: embeddings = NEFTune
    (2310.05914) verbatim + 2026 refinement (2605.23171); attention = Attention-
    Drop (2504.12088), whose "Blurred Attention Smoothing" IS Gaussian
    convolution over attention logits (DropAttention/DropHead predate it);
    noise-in-KD = SMART (2310.01209), Noisy Feature Distillation, SoKD, Noisy
    Self-KD (2009.07032). MECHANISM KILL (stronger than the citations): NEFTune
    works because noise stops overfitting to instruction-data specifics so the
    model falls back on its PRETRAINED knowledge — a from-scratch 3.4M student
    has no pretrained prior to fall back on, so the engine behind the headline
    number is structurally absent. Predicted null.
    TWO POINTS FOR IT (own logs): these students DO overfit in the test regime
    (task 62.7% -> 59.2% across epochs @2048 ex), and measured teacher entropy
    is 0.021 (near one-hot), so KD contributes almost no smoothing of its own —
    the usual "KD already regularizes" objection is weak here.
    Sliver: "does noise regularization transfer when the pretrained prior it
    relies on is absent?" = same species as S1-S4. A clean negative is
    defensible; not a paper.

31. Step-alternating losses (every 2nd/3rd step KL) vs ratio mixing; "which is
    better, loss annealing or loss-step annealing?"
    DEAD — killed by algebra, no citation needed. Picking KL w.p. p and CE w.p.
    1-p gives E[grad] = p*grad_KL + (1-p)*grad_CE = the gradient of the
    p-weighted mixed loss. Alternating at 66/33 IS mixing at 0.66/0.33 in
    expectation — not a different objective, a higher-variance estimator of the
    same one. No prior work compares them in KD, but that absence is evidence
    the question is trivial, not that it is open. Also re-enters #1 (loss-
    schedule family: Hinton/Annealing-KD/CTKD/DKD; Menon ICML 2021 predicts
    CE~=KL) which we already shot down, and our own runs already measured CE~=KL.
    ONLY live corner: under Adam the equivalence breaks (second-moment
    statistics differ), so it is an optimizer-interaction footnote. Ablation
    column at most. Do NOT write a thesis on this.

32. MoE viability sub-200M when distilling FROM a dense teacher
    PREMISE REFUTED as posed — "Slicing and Dicing" (2605.11689) reports ALL
    MoEs underperform dense at 10M/20M ACTIVE params under Chinchilla-optimal
    data, and only overtake at ~100-400x tokens per active param (~20x
    Chinchilla); Joint MoE Scaling Laws (2502.05172) and 2509.23678 agree. We
    are at 3.4M-14M on 2k-50k examples — orders of magnitude below the crossover.
    Compounded by #10 (edge is MEMORY-bound, 2606.21428; MoE must hold every
    expert while using a fraction = worst trade on device). Live direction is
    the REVERSE: MoE->dense (2605.28207), KD *for* MoE LMs (2502.12947).
    *** SLIVER WORTH KEEPING (best of batch 4) *** — every one of those scaling
    laws trains on HARD next-token targets. Distillation supplies a full teacher
    distribution = far more information per example, and MoE's documented
    small-scale weakness is precisely DATA HUNGER. So: "does distillation's
    higher information-per-example move the MoE viability threshold DOWN, and by
    how much?" Unanswered by the papers that set the threshold, mechanistically
    motivated, headline either way ("soft targets buy MoE N-fold data
    efficiency" / "no, the threshold is architectural"). Most expensive build on
    the board.

33. Component scan addendum (shallow, not systematic) — not yet on cluster F:
    Selective Attention (2410.02703, parameter-free, moderate-scale ablations
    only) and 2-simplicial attention (2507.02754, trilinear, genuine new
    component, nontrivial kernels). Neither displaces F2 Canon as the strongest
    scale-down piece, and Canon's closing-window note still stands.

34. Post-gen loss->metric dissociation (the top-k table: anneal wins top-20,
    pure KL wins overlap + mean rank, pure CE tanks mean rank 1069)
    SCOPED AS PHENOMENON. Each loss wins the metric nearest its own objective,
    near-tautologically: CE supervises only the target token, so tail ordering
    is unsupervised (rank 1069); KL supervises the full ranking (rank 419-486).
    That tail structure IS "dark knowledge" (Hinton 2015), and the target/
    non-target split is the DKD decomposition; mixed-beats-pure on accuracy is
    Hinton's alpha-mixing. DATA CORRECTIONS found on re-read: the 3-row table
    omitted the purekl-t5to3 arm (67.19% top-20 / 37.67% overlap / rank 418.6),
    which is the temperature-matched comparison — with it the anneal's top-20
    lead shrinks 4.7pp -> 1.9pp and pure KL wins BOTH distribution metrics.
    n=1 per arm except anneal (n=2, replicates 0.27pp apart; 47,911 scored
    steps/eval so measurement noise is tiny; seed noise unknown).
    *** KEEP AS THESIS FIGURE, not as research push *** — measured 2026-08-26
    from stored logits: teacher entropy post-gen = 1.059 nats vs reddit 0.023 /
    math 0.0043 (46x). Losses separate EXACTLY on the one domain with dark
    knowledge, tying the CE~=KL null (#1) to teacher entropy: "loss choice
    matters in proportion to available dark knowledge, shown across 3 domains
    on one pipeline". Theory-predicted (Menon 2021), clean own-numbers demo.

35. Hungarian math tutor via prompt internalization (teacher gets Hungarian
    phrases/persona; cheap intelligible Hungarian tutor)
    COMPONENTS ALL OWNED; COMBINATION UNCLAIMED BUT A SUBSTITUTION INSTANCE.
    Prompt internalization = mature line (context distillation, Askell 2021;
    Generative Prompt Internalization 2411.15927; Microsoft OPCD 2026 bakes
    system prompts into weights). Small math distillation crowded (Orca-Math
    et al.). Hungarian adaptation ACTIVE: PULI family, PULI LlumiX/Trio chat,
    and Racka (2601.01244, Jan 2026) — Qwen-3 4B + LoRA continual pretrain,
    swaps 32k tokenizer entries -> 47% lower Hungarian fertility. I.e. the
    "Hungarian-adapted vocabulary" axis (where reduced-vocab would finally
    have real-world motivation) was claimed at 4B THIS YEAR by a Hungarian
    academic group. No hit for a Hungarian math TUTOR specifically.
    Journal-weak; TDK-PLAUSIBLE as engineering artifact (local value, and
    agglutinative Hungarian makes it technically non-trivial — Racka's 47%
    is the effect size). FATAL IN CURRENT PIPELINE: from-scratch students
    cannot produce free-form Hungarian — best post-gen student terminates
    naturally on 50.5% and has 4.7% autoregressive token accuracy IN ENGLISH;
    fluency from scratch needs TinyStories-scale data (millions of examples =
    months of local teacher generation). Teacher cap: Gemma 3 4B Hungarian is
    shaky; distilling broken Hungarian = confidently broken tutor. The
    buildable version — internalize the persona into a pretrained small base
    (Gemma 3 270M, phone-capable) with a 27B/Racka teacher — is a FORK of the
    thesis, not an extension (shares almost no pipeline).


36. Oscillating directional annealing (KL/CE ratio descends with +/-10%
    cycles superimposed)
    SCHEDULE FAMILY OWNED + NO MECHANISM HERE. Cyclical KL-weight annealing is
    Fu et al. NAACL 2019 (1903.10145) + follow-ups (2003.01889) — famous VAE
    trick. It worked there because of a REAL pathology (KL vanishing/posterior
    collapse: one loss kills the other's signal; cycles re-open it). Our two
    losses have no such antagonism: CE's target IS the teacher argmax, and we
    MEASURED CE~=KL on the near-one-hot domains — oscillating +/-10% between
    two nearly identical gradients perturbs nothing. On post-gen the losses
    differ but no collapse-like pathology exists to fix. Inherits half of #31:
    cycle-averaged it IS the base schedule; residual is a second-order
    trajectory effect (nonzero under Adam, tiny). One notch above #31 (cycles
    are deterministic, LR warm-restart precedent exists) but still an ablation
    cell, not a contribution. Predicted null within seed noise.

37. ENTROPY-GATED DISTILLATION — "the KD-vs-CE gap is a function of teacher
    entropy; measure the dose-response curve causally"
    SURVIVED LAP 6 — STRONG. Menon (2005.10419) implies it in theory, never
    measures it. Adaptive per-token divergence switching USES the insight
    (2603.07079, 2605.01732) but never states the law. 2510.15516 supports
    dark knowledge via a DATASET-SIZE manipulation — different axis. My own
    spot-check found the real threat the agents missed: Muller 1906.02629
    (label-smoothed teachers distill worse) IS a causal entropy manipulation
    — but confounded (label smoothing reshapes logit geometry / erases
    inter-class similarity AND requires retraining the teacher). Our
    instrument avoids the confound: re-temper the SAME stored logits post
    hoc (ranking preserved, only entropy dialed), same data, same eval,
    20-50 seeds per point. Natural anchor already measured: 0.0043 / 0.023 /
    1.06 nats across domains with the tie/separation pattern. CAVEAT: the
    H->0 endpoint is trivial by construction (at tau->0 KD literally equals
    our CE, which already targets the teacher trace); the contribution is
    the SHAPE of the curve and the metric split (post-gen shows KL wins
    distribution metrics but NOT top-20 — entropy may gate fidelity, not
    task accuracy: that refinement is the non-obvious part).

38. DIVERGENCE ZOO AT SUB-30M, OFFLINE — "does the reverse-KL advantage
    survive with no pretrained prior?"
    SURVIVED LAP 6 — STRONG. MiniLLM (2306.08543), GKD (2306.13649),
    DistiLLM 1/2 (2402.03898, 2503.07067), f-distill (2307.15190): all
    students >=120M and PRETRAINED, mostly needing on-policy sampling. With
    full stored logits every token-level f-divergence (fwd/rev KL, JS,
    skew, TVD) is computable OFFLINE and exactly — no sampling noise, no
    pretraining confound. Mechanism question with real content: mode-seeking
    assumes a capable student choosing among modes; a from-scratch 14M
    student may need mode-COVERING first. Could flip the accepted ranking.
    Shares all infrastructure with #37. A well-error-barred "the divergence
    choice is a large-model phenomenon" null is itself publishable.

39. MULTI-TOKEN PREDICTION UNDER KD — "does KD move the MTP crossover?"
    SURVIVED LAP 6 — STRONG (best architecture-flavored candidate yet on
    this ledger). Meta 2404.19737 documents a clean crossover: MTP HURTS
    below ~1.3B under hard labels (300M/600M worse, gains only >=1.3B) and
    says so explicitly. All MTP+distillation work is about speeding up big
    pretrained models (2602.06019, 2603.23911, KAT-V1 2507.08297 at 40B).
    Nobody has trained from-scratch <100M MTP under teacher-KL. Mechanism:
    accepted small-scale failure story is signal sparsity of one-hot future
    labels — and we have the teacher's FULL distribution at t+1/t+2 already
    on disk, free. Either sign is a result: "soft targets rescue MTP at
    tiny scale" or "MTP failure is capacity, not signal". Implementation:
    one extra head + one shifted KL term, days. Testbed must be post-gen
    (classification outputs too short/deterministic).

40. KD-GROKKING PHASE DIAGRAM — "do soft labels compress, eliminate, or
    transmit the memorization plateau?"
    SURVIVED LAP 6 — STRONG with RACE RISK. 2511.04760 (Nov 2025) showed KD
    from a grokked teacher accelerates grokking CROSS-distribution — but
    (full text checked by agent) no same-distribution analysis, no
    temperature/alpha sweep, no pre-grok vs post-grok teacher ablation, no
    seed counts. 2607.05104 has the many-seed statistics (n=10, 12K params)
    but hard-label only. The sharp probe: distill from teacher checkpoints
    taken PRE-grok / mid / POST-grok — a clean causal test of what soft
    labels actually carry. Label smoothing is the essential control arm
    (2602.12039, 2501.04697: uniform soft targets != teacher soft targets).
    Needs one new tiny algorithmic domain (modular addition) + in-sandbox
    teacher; grokking hyperparameter archaeology first. Race risk: the
    2511.04760 group is the obvious one to extend; 6-12 month window.

41. VARIANCE-CONTRACTION AUDIT — "does KD shrink the seed lottery?"
    SURVIVED LAP 6 — STRONG, highest feasibility (re-runs existing configs).
    2305.17581 (NeurIPS 2023) PROVES KD partially reduces gradient variance
    — a theory prediction never tested at the outcome-distribution level.
    2510.12615 (3,900-model audit) tests final-accuracy means only, CV
    classification. Nobody has published sigma2_KD/sigma2_CE per metric
    across alpha and temperature at 30-50 seeds, nor trajectory statistics
    (time-to-validity, time-to-task-accuracy). Framing discipline required:
    this is "testing a NeurIPS theory claim where only we can afford to",
    never "we ran more seeds". Natural methodology backbone for #37-40.

42. MUON AT TINY SCALE UNDER KL — ADEQUATE-to-STRONG, conditional on
    pre-registered non-obvious hypotheses: (a) seed-variance contraction
    (nobody measures optimizer effects on seed spread), (b) rare-class F1
    interaction (2509.26030: Muon wins on tail associative memory; KD soft
    targets put mass exactly on rare tokens), (c) temperature interaction.
    2601.09865 states verbatim that Muon x logit-distillation is
    "underexplored". As a plain speed benchmark it degrades to WEAK.
    ~50 lines of PyTorch; AdamW baseline must get an equal LR sweep.

43. STRUCTURE-BEFORE-CONTENT ACQUISITION ORDER UNDER KD + token-class KL
    decomposition — ADEQUATE-to-STRONG. Acquisition-order literature is all
    hard-label pretraining (2309.07311, 2109.06096, 2205.11758). Our own
    100%-valid-JSON-at-60%-task gap is the phenomenon. The unclaimed piece:
    split training KL into STRUCTURE-token vs CONTENT-token components from
    stored logits. Hypothesis: teacher entropy ~0 on structure tokens means
    KD==CE there; all KD value localizes on content tokens. NOTE: this is
    #37 AT WITHIN-SEQUENCE GRANULARITY — same law, second scale. Frequency
    confound must be controlled (structure tokens are more frequent).

68. BINARY OUTPUT HEAD — represent tokens as bit strings, output head has
    2 logits, distill dark knowledge into the bits    [checked 2026-09-06]
    METHOD DEAD (SAVES NOTHING HERE); ONE SHARP MEASUREMENT SURVIVES.
    MEASURED PARAMETER BREAKDOWN OF OUR OWN 3.4M STUDENT
    (hidden_dim 48, 3 layers, input vocab 68,328, output vocab 525):
        input embedding    3,279,744   96.00%
        transformer body     110,928    3.25%
        output head           25,725    0.75%
        TOTAL              3,416,397
    Binarizing the output head (525 -> 10 bits) saves 25,235 params =
    0.74% OF THE MODEL. The motivation ("vocab is a huge chunk of a sub-
    100M model") is correct in general and correct about the INPUT side,
    but points at the wrong half: the output head is 0.75%.
    THE MECHANISM HAS A NAME: hierarchical softmax (Morin & Bengio 2005;
    word2vec 1301.3781 / 1310.4546 / 1411.2738, Huffman-tree variant).
    ~20 years old.
    THE UNCLAIMED CRUX (worth keeping even though the method is dead):
    hierarchical softmax with PER-NODE parameters is exact but has V-1
    internal nodes, so it saves nothing. A single shared head predicting
    bits INDEPENDENTLY saves parameters precisely because it cannot
    represent correlations between bits — and the dark knowledge it
    destroys equals exactly the TOTAL CORRELATION (multi-information)
    between the bits under the teacher's distribution.
    THE SAVINGS AND THE LOSS ARE THE SAME QUANTITY.
    A targeted search returned explicit NON-COVERAGE of "independent bits,
    total correlation, or hierarchical softmax" in the KD literature —
    meaningful negative evidence, not just absence of a hit.
    ENTROPY TIE-IN (third independent appearance of the law): a near-point
    mass factorizes almost perfectly (total correlation ~ 0), so at
    reddit's H=0.021 binarization is nearly FREE, while at post-gen's
    exp(H)=2.88 it is expensive. Prediction: binary heads cost nothing
    exactly where the teacher is confident.
    FREE EXPERIMENT, ZERO TRAINING: fit the best product-of-Bernoullis to
    each stored 525-way teacher distribution under a code assignment,
    report the KL gap, sweep assignments (random vs Huffman vs semantic
    clustering).
    WHERE THE PARAMETERS ACTUALLY ARE (input side, already occupied):
    hash/bloom embeddings (Serra & Karatzoglou 2017), product
    quantization, ALBERT factorized embedding (1909.11942), byte- and
    character-level models. ALBERT-style factorization of our own
    embedding at rank 8 gives ~546K total, an 83% cut, ~1 line at
    transformer.py:119.
    FILTER CHECK (#60): method-shaped -> dead. Tally 27/27.

    X-Token (2605.21699), CDM (2025.findings-acl.419), TokAlign
    (2506.03523), CTPD (2601.11865).
    TWO ERRORS IN THE FRAMING, INDEPENDENT OF THE SCOOP:
    (a) "redistribute programmatically" is the OPEN PROBLEM, not
        bookkeeping. A merged student token spans MULTIPLE teacher steps,
        so there is no teacher distribution at that position to copy — its
        probability is a marginal over every teacher token sequence that
        spells the string. That is why five separate 2025-26 papers attack
        it with optimal transport and byte interfaces.
    (b) "can still output everything the teacher could" is FALSE. A merged
        vocabulary is closed under nothing; expressivity needs byte or
        character escapes, and those escapes are where accuracy dies — on
        exactly the rare inputs.
    CORRECTION TO A CLAIM MADE IN-SESSION: "our 3.4M student is 96%
    lookup table" is a striking number but it is NOT novel — the
    literature states plainly that embeddings are 45-62% of GPT-2 and that
    the effect is "especially pronounced in small models." Our 96% is an
    extreme instance of a published fact. Do not build a claim on it.
    FROM-SCRATCH IS A HOLE IN THE WRONG DIRECTION: every prior initializes
    the student from a pretrained checkpoint, which is WHY FVT needs
    copy-shared-tokens / average-sub-tokens init and why the Russian paper
    needs alignment tricks. We have no source embeddings, so none of that
    machinery applies — the hard part is ABSENT, not solved. "We did not
    need their method because our setting is easier" is not a
    contribution. Same shape as the surviving sliver in #65.
    WHAT ACTUALLY SURVIVES (measurement-shaped, so it passes #60):
    MERGING TOKENS IS AN ENTROPY TRANSFORMATION. By the chain rule the
    sequence entropy is conserved, so packing the same content into fewer
    steps RAISES per-token teacher entropy; splitting finer lowers it.
    Tokenizer granularity is therefore a free, continuous dial that sweeps
    teacher entropy with the DOMAIN AND TEACHER HELD FIXED — which is the
    fix for #66's n=2 problem, with no second teacher and no data
    regeneration.
    THE UNCOMFORTABLE COROLLARY, WHICH IS THE FINDING: aggressive domain
    merging makes the model smaller AND the distillation harder, because
    it destroys the near-one-hot teacher (H=0.021) that currently makes KL
    cheap. Compression bought is paid for in distillation difficulty, at a
    measurable exchange rate. SAME SHAPE AS #68 — second independent
    instance, which makes it a law rather than an anecdote.
    NEAREST PRIOR ON THE SURVIVING PART, AND IT DOES NOT REACH: Compute
    Optimal Tokenization (2605.01188) and token-granularity-vs-surprisal
    (2412.11940) vary granularity but never touch distillation; EGAD
    (2605.01732) uses entropy as a per-token weighting heuristic, not as a
    domain property, and does not vary vocabulary or tokenization at all.
    FILTER CHECK (#60): method-shaped -> dead, exactly as predicted before
    searching. Tally 28/28. Every measurement-shaped entry still alive.
    ONE-LINE PAPER FORM: at sub-1M scale the embedding IS the model, so
    tokenizer granularity is simultaneously the dominant compression lever
    and the dominant control on teacher entropy — the compression you buy
    is paid for in distillation difficulty at a measurable exchange rate.

========================================================================
HOW TO WRITE AN ENTRY (added 2026-09-06 — read before appending)
========================================================================
Entries 1-19 are three lines. Entries 55-69 run to seventy. That drift
happened because the file quietly took on a second job. Keep the two
apart:

THE LEDGER (this file) is a KILL RECORD. One entry =

    N. IDEA NAME IN CAPS                            [checked YYYY-MM-DD]
       2-4 sentences: what the idea was, in plain terms.

       2-4 sentences: verdict, who owns it, the decisive finding, arXiv
       IDs inline. Name the one surviving opening if there is one.

Nothing else. If an entry needs more room, what it needs room for is
almost never the idea — it is a FINDING, and findings go elsewhere.

A FINDING is anything that outlives the dead idea and would be cited in
the thesis on its own: our own measured numbers, a methodological trap, a
formula, a cost wall, a live claim. Those belong in findings.txt, keyed
F-<entry><letter>, cross-referenced from the entry in one clause.

EXCEPTIONS — entries allowed to stay long: the ones that are ALIVE
(#56, #66) and the ones carrying our own experimental data. Three or four
out of seventy, not the default.

PROVENANCE RULE (unchanged): if a paper's body could not be read, say so
in the entry, as #55 and #70 do. Never let a search-engine summary pass
as a read paper.
