*** ANCHOR THEME — REDUCED OUTPUT VOCAB AS A MITIGATION FOR THE SOFTMAX/OUTPUT-
    RANK BOTTLENECK THAT CRIPPLES TINY MODELS ***
Multiple independent papers flag the SAME small-model weakness and LEAVE THE FIX
TO FUTURE WORK — and the untested fix is your exact knob. Likely a POSITIVE result
(reduced vocab HELPS), which is Sanyo's "beats existing on a sub-case" target.
  A1. LM Saturation / Softmax Bottleneck (2404.07647): "<1000 hidden dims ->
      degenerate representations"; mismatch of small hidden dim vs HIGH-RANK
      target dist; "we leave exploration of [softmax alternatives] to future
      work." Never tests reducing output vocab. -> reduced vocab lowers target
      rank = the untested mitigation. [ANCHOR, check first]
  A2. LM Head is a Gradient Bottleneck (2603.10145): softmax bottleneck "destroys
      >95% of gradient norm"; future work = "softmax alternatives that preserve
      gradient flow." Reduced vocab = a fix they list but never run. Measure
      gradient-norm retention vs vocab size.
  A3. Distillation Traps & Guards (2604.18963): full-vocab KL "tail domination"
      -> low-capacity students "mode-average / cover entire support." ~525 vocab
      directly removes the tail trap. Does reduced vocab raise fidelity?
  A4. Scaling Laws with Vocabulary (2407.13623, NeurIPS'24): "limited vocabulary
      may constrain performance... do not empirically validate"; floor 33M ->
      sub-10M reduced-vocab corner unvalidated.

  >>> HOSTILE CHECK VERDICT (2026-07-16): *** SURVIVES *** — FIRST hard-pass
  survivor of the whole hunt. Nobody claims reduced OUTPUT vocab relieves the
  softmax bottleneck (bottleneck papers -> richer decoders; reduced-vocab papers
  -> only param savings; tail-KL papers -> loss reweighting). The DUAL move
  (shrink target rank via task vocab so a low-d student can express it) is
  unclaimed + untested. Nearest paper 2603.10145, then 2404.07647.
  FRAMING GUARDRAILS: (1) claim the MITIGATION+MEASUREMENT, not the phenomenon
  (cite 2603.10145 / 2404.07647 as mechanism you operationalize); (2) DROP the
  "uninformative tail dominates" wording (2602.20816 contradicts it) -> reframe
  as "matched-capacity KL is easier when target rank <= student expressible rank."
  EXPERIMENT (Sanyo implement+measure): matched body (same d, layers, data) x
  vary output V {~525, 2k, 8k, ~26k}; measure per V: (1) head-vs-body gradient-
  norm fraction (2603.10145 metric); (2) target log-prob-matrix rank vs student
  rank ~d; (3) hidden-state singular-value spectrum / isotropy (degeneracy);
  (4) distillation fidelity + task accuracy. PREDICT: smaller V -> more gradient
  reaches body, target rank below student ceiling, less degeneracy, better
  fidelity/accuracy at matched capacity = reduced vocab lets tiny models ESCAPE
  the bottleneck = positive headline result. THIS IS THE ANCHOR/HEADLINE.
