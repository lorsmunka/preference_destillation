LOSS / SCHEDULE FAMILY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.  LOSS-SCHEDULE SWEEP                            [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Sweep the KL/CE mixing ratio, the temperature annealing schedule, and
    the direction of annealing (KL→CE against CE→KL), looking for a
    recipe that beats the default.

    SCOOPED. The mechanism is Hinton, Annealing-KD, CTKD and DKD, and the
    CE≈KL outcome is already predicted theoretically by Menon (ICML 2021).
    Our own runs then measured CE≈KL directly, so the sweep confirms a
    published prediction rather than finding anything.

2.  SHARPNESS CROSSOVER                            [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Claim that KL is marginal on sharp domains and decisive on free-form
    ones, so the value of the loss choice crosses over with how peaked the
    teacher is.

    SCOOPED by near-identical concurrent ICML 2026 work: Bridge-Garden
    (2605.26246) and EGAD (2605.01732), both entropy-keyed, both verified
    real. The observation survives only as our own cross-domain figure
    (see #34), not as a claim.

3.  CE→KL REVERSE-DIRECTION ANNEALING              [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Anneal from cross-entropy toward KL rather than the usual direction,
    on the grounds that the reverse direction is the untested one.

    WOUNDED and close to trivial. "Anneal toward the distribution you want
    at the end" is almost a tautology, and the mixing-beats-pure result is
    Bridge-Garden's. Same family as #1, #31, #36 and #58, all ruled
    scoop-heavy.

31. STEP-ALTERNATING LOSSES                        [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Instead of mixing KL and CE by a ratio, alternate them by step — every
    second or third step uses KL — and ask whether loss-step annealing
    beats loss annealing.

    DEAD by algebra, no citation needed. Picking KL with probability p
    gives E[grad] = p·grad_KL + (1−p)·grad_CE, which is exactly the
    gradient of the p-weighted mixed loss: alternation is a
    higher-variance estimator of the same objective, not a different one.
    The only live corner is that Adam's second-moment statistics break the
    equivalence — an optimizer footnote, an ablation column at most.

36. OSCILLATING DIRECTIONAL ANNEALING              [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Descend the KL/CE ratio while superimposing ±10% cycles, borrowing the
    cyclical-annealing trick from VAEs.

    SCOOPED and mechanism-free here. Cyclical KL-weight annealing is Fu et
    al. NAACL 2019 (1903.10145) plus follow-ups (2003.01889); it worked
    there because of a real pathology (KL vanishing / posterior collapse)
    that our two losses do not have, since CE's target *is* the teacher
    argmax. Inherits #31's kill: cycle-averaged it is the base schedule.
    Predicted null within seed noise.

58. ENTROPY-BASED LOSS ANNEALING                   [checked 2026-08-31]
    Key the KL/CE ratio to the teacher's entropy at each token rather than
    to the training step, so the loss adapts to how much dark knowledge is
    actually present.

    DEAD — seven papers, 2023→2026: TSLD (NeurIPS 2023), EA-KD
    (2311.13621), EGAD (2605.01732, our idea verbatim), Entropy-Aware
    On-Policy (2603.07079, ICLR 2026), 2608.14685, SEAD (2606.28562), TIP
    (2604.14084). Also inert on our domains by construction — an
    entropy-keyed weight is a constant when entropy is constant, and
    reddit and math are flat. SALVAGE: run it on post-gen only as a
    confirmation arm for our own law, with reddit as the no-op control;
    never write it up as a proposed method.


VOCABULARY FAMILY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.  OUTPUT-VOCAB FLOOR / COLLAPSE CURVE            [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Find how small the output vocabulary can get before KL stops carrying
    useful information — a collapse curve for dark knowledge against
    vocabulary size.

    WOUNDED. Kolesnikova (2205.02340) already plots KD quality against
    vocabulary size and found no clean collapse, and Subclass Distillation
    owns "few classes starve dark knowledge." Note this same paper returns
    as a direct owner in #69.

5.  BEST AUXILIARY-TOKEN SELECTION                 [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Choose which auxiliary tokens to keep by ranking them on teacher
    probability mass rather than by frequency or hand-selection.

    SCOOPED. Token selection by teacher mass is Sparse Logit Sampling
    (2503.16870), Selective KD (2602.01395) and the top-K logit
    distillation line.

6.  RETAINED-MASS PREDICTOR                        [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    A cheap forward pass measures how much teacher probability mass
    survives vocabulary truncation, and predicts where KL will collapse —
    so you know the safe vocabulary size without training.

    MEASURED, no-go, on our own machine: both sharp and soft domains
    retain ~99% of mass at their operating vocabulary, so retained mass
    discriminates nothing. Entropy is the real discriminator. The metric
    is also already named ("Don't Ignore the Tail", 2602.20816, ICML
    2026). Same failure shape as #70 — see F-70b.

17. PROGRESSIVE OUTPUT-VOCAB REDUCTION             [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Shrink the output vocabulary during training as a curriculum: start
    wide so the student sees the full distribution, end narrow.

    SCOOPED. DeepKD (2505.15133, Dynamic Top-K Masking) and PCD
    (2505.24310, C2FL branch) already schedule KD output-support size
    during training in both directions, as of May 2025. That we schedule
    it in the shrinking direction is true but does not help once the
    size-schedule axis itself is claimed; only a physically-shrinking-head
    engineering delta survives.

18. LEARNED / DIFFERENTIABLE OUTPUT VOCABULARY     [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Learn which tokens the output head should keep, end to end, instead of
    selecting them by probability mass.

    SCOOPED and benefit-null. VVD (NAACL 2019) owns
    learned-selection-beats-frequency on input embeddings, so porting it
    to a KD output head is an old technique in a new layer. Theory then
    says the gain over mass-ranking is roughly zero, because the KL loss
    is already mass-weighted.

15. REDUCED INPUT-VOCAB ON MATH / POST-GEN         [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Close the thesis gap by applying the reduced input vocabulary to the
    other two domains as well.

    MATHEMATICALLY INERT — dropping unused embedding rows cannot change
    any output. It is compression, not behaviour: it closes a gap in the
    writeup and claims nothing.


PROMPT / CONTEXT FAMILY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.  PROMPT INTERNALIZATION INTO A TINY STUDENT     [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Bake the task prompt into the weights of a from-scratch student, so it
    performs the task without being told what the task is.

    WOUNDED. The mechanism is Snell 2022, "Learning by Distilling
    Context", verbatim, with SKIntern, PromptIntern and Gist Tokens piling
    on. Only the tiny from-scratch scale is new.

8.  CO-COMPRESSION INTERACTION                     [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Ask whether prompt internalization still works when the output
    vocabulary is simultaneously starved to ~500 tokens and the student is
    trained from scratch — the joint corner of two compressions.

    ALIVE (thin). The joint corner is genuinely unmeasured, but it is an
    existence proof and a combination rather than a new mechanism.

24. TEACHER-PROMPT AS THE DOMINANT LEVER           [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Treat the teacher's prompt as the main thing that determines student
    quality, and document the pitfalls of internalizing it.

    SCOOPED. Snell 2022 (2209.15189) is the exact setup including the
    capacity ceiling; the pitfalls paper already exists (GenPI 2411.15927,
    NAACL 2025); prompt-as-lever is Distilling Step-by-Step and SCoTD; the
    prompt-vs-generator decomposition is 2604.13977. Sliver: prompt-vs-
    recipe attribution inside the reduced-vocab regime, a measurement only.

65. MULTIPLE PROMPT INTERNALIZATION                [checked 2026-09-05]
    Have the teacher answer the same input 4-5 times under different
    prompts, then distil the ensemble, on the theory that this
    manufactures dark knowledge where a single prompt gives none.

    METHOD SCOOPED — 2206.13214 uses the phrase "multi-prompt
    distillation" on a subjective task, 2506.03857 and 2603.21404 are
    adjacent, and CLIP's 80 templates is the upstream canon. One live
    thing survives: prompt-ensembling work says averaging HELPS while
    multi-teacher work (2302.07215, NeurIPS 2024 ensemble-then-distill)
    says averaging makes knowledge SHALLOWER, and our entropy law predicts
    which regime applies. Budget-neutral to run (N/5 inputs × 5 prompts).
    HARD GATE FIRST: 5 prompt variants on ~50 reddit examples — if every
    variant returns the same argmax, nothing is manufactured and it dies
    free.


SYSTEMS / DEPLOYMENT FAMILY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

9.  TOOL-CALL TUTOR                                [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    A tiny distilled expert that emits calculator or tool calls instead of
    computing answers itself, packaged as a maths tutor.

    SCOOPED — every verb is owned: Toolformer, Calc-X/Calcformers, PAL and
    PoT, Agent Distillation (2505.17612).

10. CHAIN OF TINY EXPERTS VS MoE ON EDGE           [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Compare a chain of small distilled experts against a mixture of
    experts for on-device deployment.

    SCOOPED and the premise is refuted. NVIDIA 2506.02153 and
    Mixture-of-Modular-Experts 2407.19610 own the comparison, and
    2606.21428 shows edge deployment is memory-bound rather than
    compute-bound — which kills the entire "FLOPs is the constraint"
    framing the idea rests on.

19. SHARED BODY + SWAPPABLE PER-DOMAIN HEADS       [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    One tiny shared transformer body with a reduced-vocabulary head per
    domain, swapped at load time, so a device holds one body and many
    tasks.

    SCOOPED and the value proposition is killed. Classic MTL
    shared-trunk/heads (MT-DNN, HydraNet, Apple 2024, MT-DNN+KD 1904.09482)
    owns the structure; only reduced-vocab-head-as-unit at sub-10M is
    unclaimed. Negative transfer at tiny scale (Stem Cell Hypothesis,
    EMNLP 2021) then predicts independent experts beat the shared body,
    removing the memory saving that motivated it.

47. BAKED-IN "WILL I BE WRONG" HEAD                [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    An auxiliary head trained alongside the student that flags likely
    incorrect answers from internal features, ideally saying why.

    MECHANISM OWNED AT EVERY LAYER: ConfidNet (1910.04851, NeurIPS 2019)
    plus TPAMI follow-up owns the aux error head, Lookback Lens
    (2407.07071) owns wrongness-from-attention, and P(True) (Kadavath
    2207.05221) owns self-judged correctness — with a scale finding that
    cuts against us. SALVAGE as a demo feature, not a claim: maths gives
    free wrongness labels, and a logistic probe on the final hidden state
    is a weekend of work.

48. PHONE COMMITTEE                                [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Three or four geometrically different tiny students on one device,
    rotating to a peer whenever the active one is unsure.

    MECHANISM OWNED — learning-to-defer with multiple experts (2210.16955,
    Mao NeurIPS 2023, 2310.14774, 2506.20650), routing/cascading survey
    2603.04445, edge-cloud cascades 2512.20012. ONE SPRINKLE SURVIVES:
    diversify students BY THE DISTILLATION DIVERGENCE ITSELF (reverse-KL
    mode-seeking vs forward-KL mode-covering) and ask whether they make
    complementary or nested errors — two-sided, and nearly free on top of
    #38's runs. See F-48a for the diversity-source shootout.

49. AUTOMATED COMMITTEE PIPELINE                   [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Overproduce ten diverse distills, select the four with maximum
    correct-answer coverage, train a per-model wrongness gate, ship four
    plus one, configurable.

    EVERY STAGE HAS NAMED PRIOR ART — Caruana ICML 2004 ensemble selection
    from libraries, submodular greedy max-coverage, META-DES (Pattern
    Recognition 2015) for per-instance competence gating — so it is not
    presentable as a method. But the central comparison IS contested:
    Kondratyuk 2005.00570 (ensembles of smaller beat single large at
    matched FLOPs) against "Deep ensembles work, but are they necessary?"
    (NeurIPS 2022), both at vision scale and unmeasured in the tiny
    regime. Conditionally TDK-worthy as an applied entry; see F-49a for
    the execution requirements that decide whether it wins or embarrasses.

51. MODEL-WITHIN-A-MODEL DRAFTER                   [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    A small nested submodel inside the student, frozen or alternately
    trained, used as a speculative-decoding drafter for the full model.

    SCOOPED thoroughly. MatFormer (2310.07707) is a nested Matryoshka
    submodel trained jointly and used explicitly as a speculative drafter;
    LayerSkip (2404.16710), Draft & Verify (2309.08168), Medusa
    (2401.10774), EAGLE (2401.15077), Kangaroo (2404.18911) and
    DistillSpec (2310.08461) fill the rest. No sandbox sliver either:
    speculative decoding pays when the TARGET is expensive, and a 14M
    model is the drafter, not the target.

67. SPECULATIVE DECODING WITH THE FIRST LAYERS     [checked 2026-09-06]
    Use the first layer or two of a three-layer student to draft tokens
    that the remaining layers then verify.

    DEAD — exactly scooped. LayerSkip (2404.16710, ACL 2024, Meta) is this
    idea by name: layer dropout plus a shared early-exit head so early
    layers draft and the rest verify, up to 2.16× on LLaMA. SWIFT
    (2410.06916) and Component-Aware Self-Speculative (2605.01106) sit
    beside it, and #27 had already killed the distillation angle.


INTERPRETABILITY / PROBING FAMILY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

11. CE-VS-KL REPRESENTATION COMPARISON             [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Two students reach the same accuracy under different losses — are they
    the same model inside?

    SCOOPED. Müller 2019 (soft targets change geometry at equal accuracy),
    Stanton 2021 (accuracy ≠ internal fidelity) and Distilled Circuits
    (2505.10822, generative, 2025) own it. Only accuracy-matched seed
    baseline hygiene and the tiny sandbox survive.

12. CALIBRATION TRANSFER + DEFERRAL CASCADE        [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Ask whether calibration survives distillation into a tiny
    reduced-vocabulary student, and use the calibrated confidence to defer
    hard inputs upward.

    SCOOPED hard — calibration transfer via KD (2302.11472), teacher
    calibration (2508.20224), Gatekeeper cascade (2502.19335), Müller
    2019. The closed-vocabulary "clean ECE" pitch is also backwards:
    classification is already the cleanest setting for calibration.

28. WATCHER MODEL FROM ATTENTION PATTERNS          [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    A second model predicts whether the student is about to be wrong by
    reading its attention patterns.

    SCOOPED and saturated 2024-2026: Lookback Lens (2407.07071), spectral
    attention features (2502.17598), question-only probes that predict
    accuracy before generation (2509.10625), self-failure circuits
    (2512.20578), entropy-trace monitoring (2601.09001) — and the deferral
    use case already died as #12. At 3 layers and 525 tokens, plain output
    entropy probably predicts errors anyway, more cheaply than any probe.

50. ATTENTION-SIGNATURE TOOL                       [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Characterize models by how they attend, and use attention diversity to
    select or route committee members without needing labels.

    SURVIVES THE OLD MECH-INTERP KILL BUT FACES TWO NEW ONES. Jain &
    Wallace kills attention-as-explanation, not attention-as-predictive-
    feature, so the reframe is legitimate — but computing attention
    divergence requires running both models, which yields OUTPUT
    DISAGREEMENT for free, a closer and stronger baseline; and circuit
    seed-instability (2510.00845, ~21% feature reproducibility over three
    seeds) predicts the signatures are seed-dominated. DECISION TEST, an
    afternoon on #38's runs: the tool exists only if attention divergence
    predicts true error complementarity AND adds over output disagreement.


SCALE / CAPACITY FAMILY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

13. REASONING SCALE FLOOR                          [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Find the minimum model size and vocabulary at which reasoning
    successfully distils into a small student.

    SCOOPED. SCoTD (2306.14050) owns the question, and Teaching Arithmetic
    to Small Transformers (2307.03381) already does sub-10M FROM-SCRATCH
    verifiable multi-step arithmetic. Sub-10M is likely below the floor
    anyway, so the curve is flat at chance. One fresh variable survives:
    output-vocabulary size as a reasoning-capacity axis, rather than the
    scale floor itself.

32. MoE VIABILITY SUB-200M UNDER DISTILLATION      [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Ask whether a mixture of experts is viable below 200M parameters when
    the training signal comes from a dense teacher rather than hard labels.

    PREMISE REFUTED AS POSED — "Slicing and Dicing" (2605.11689) reports
    all MoEs underperform dense at 10-20M active parameters under
    Chinchilla-optimal data, overtaking only at ~20× Chinchilla; 2502.05172
    and 2509.23678 agree. We sit orders of magnitude below the crossover.
    SLIVER WORTH KEEPING, the best of its batch: every one of those scaling
    laws trains on hard targets, and MoE's documented small-scale weakness
    is data hunger — so "does distillation's higher information per example
    move the MoE viability threshold down, and by how much?" is unanswered
    and headline either way. Most expensive build on the board.

45. SAME-ARCHITECTURE-AS-TEACHER STUDENT           [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Ask whether a student built like its teacher wins at matched parameter
    count, given enough data and a small vocabulary.

    OWNED — it is a subfield. AKD "Search to Distill" (1911.09074) found
    exactly this (optimal students are teacher-dependent and
    structure-similar), spawning NAS-for-KD (DisWOT 2303.15678, 2206.13130,
    2001.11612); the mechanism is Abnar 2006.00555. WRONG LAB besides: at
    48 dims, 1 head, 3 layers, Gemma's architectural identity is vacuous,
    so a "mini-Gemma" arm degenerates into the vanilla student plus
    trivia. Predicted null by degeneracy, which tests nothing.

57. FRAMING CORRECTION — "NOBODY WORKS BELOW 220M" [checked 2026-08-31]
    The ledger had been leaning on the claim that the small-scale regime
    is empty.

    FALSE — DO NOT WRITE IT. TinyStories (2305.07759) is 1M-35M parameters
    with a published capability-emergence curve, dead centre of our range;
    BabyLM (CoNLL 2023/24/25) is a recurring small-budget benchmark WITH
    distillation entries (Baby Llama 2308.02019, BabyHGRN 2412.15978);
    TinyHelen 2501.00522 likewise. THE GAP IS THE CONJUNCTION: tiny-scale
    work is free-form and hard-label, distillation work is ≥0.6B and
    pretrained. TinyStories is also an ally — its grammar-before-
    consistency finding is the precedent for our validity/task gap (#43).


TEACHER-SIDE / DATA-GENERATION FAMILY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

23. TEACHER REASONS, STUDENT EMITS ONLY THE ANSWER [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Let the teacher reason at length but train the student only on the
    final answer, and ask whether teacher-side reasoning still helps.

    OWNED as a headline — Wadhwa, "Mysteries of CoT-Augmented
    Distillation" (2406.14511, EMNLP 2024) does it on small models
    including how much reasoning can be masked before collapse; Dual-Head
    (2509.21487) and the capacity-floor work (2502.18001, 2604.04037)
    surround it. SLIVER: those all put reasoning tokens IN the student
    target, whereas ours would transfer reasoning purely through the
    answer's soft distribution — see #63 for the answer, which is
    negative. Base task additionally near-scooped by 2607.08268.

25. CONFIDENCE-CURATED DISTILLATION DATA           [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Use teacher confidence or entropy to decide which examples are worth
    generating, cutting the cost of the teacher pass.

    SCOOPED. LLM on a Budget (2511.11574) reports up to 80% fewer teacher
    labels; Distill on a Diet (2606.25488) and sample selection by teacher
    signal (2404.03693) cover the rest. Sliver is a reduced-vocab
    generative measurement only.

62. DOUBLE DISTILLATION                            [checked 2026-08-31]
    Use two teachers — or take fewer labels from the expensive one and
    make up the rest with a cheap one.

    METHOD DEAD THREE WAYS: two teachers at once is textbook multi-teacher
    KD (survey 2006.05525, 2302.07215, 2605.27967); sequential is TAKD
    (1902.03393) and AMD (2407.04208); and "fewer labels from the
    expensive teacher" is LLM on a Budget (2511.11574, already our #25),
    Teaching-Assistant-in-the-Loop (2406.05322) and neural caching,
    mechanism for mechanism. SURVIVING MEASUREMENT: the exchange rate
    between cheap and expensive teacher labels, indexed by task entropy —
    2605.23857 owns teacher strength but never indexes it by entropy. See
    F-62a for the cost wall; PARKED for November, it is the follow-up
    paper.

70. TEACHER ENTROPY AS A PREDICTOR OF DISTILL QUALITY
                                                   [checked 2026-09-06]
    Measure the teacher's output distribution across sizes and families on
    a fixed domain (Gemma 4B vs Qwen 4B) and forecast, without training a
    student, which teacher yields the better small distill. A cheap pass
    over stored logits would replace a training run per teacher.

    SCOOPED, and the entropy variant was tested and LOST. GRACE
    (2511.02833) scores a teacher before any student exists — 15 teachers
    across LLaMA/OLMo/Qwen/Gemma/Phi, 1.5B-70B, 86% Spearman with
    post-distillation quality — but its score is gradient-spectral, not
    entropy, and it beats the distribution-spread baseline G-Vendi 86% to
    44%. Our hypothesis has therefore already run as someone's baseline
    and placed last: a published negative result, not an absence of
    coverage. 2604.18963 separately owns the Gemma-3-12B vs Qwen3-8B
    distillability comparison with calibration as the entropy dial (claims
    from abstract only — PDF would not yield body text, flag as in #55).
    Only opening: GRACE scores teachers globally, never per domain. See
    F-70a and F-70b.


STORAGE / PRECISION FAMILY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

16. MASS-GRADED LOGIT STORAGE PRECISION            [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Store the teacher's full distribution at graded bit-depth — more bits
    for high-mass tokens, fewer for the tail.

    SCOOPED and near-trivial: it is the KL-optimal solution, proven (ISIT
    2021/2022 companders x^{2/3} and ArcSinh) and shipped (Arcee DistillKit
    2024). The benefit is also suspect, since "Don't Ignore the Tail"
    (2602.20816) says the tail carries the signal you would be degrading.

52. THE LOGIT-STORAGE RULE                         [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Publish the required top-k for stored teacher logits as a function of
    teacher entropy — the practical recipe for how much to keep.

    ALIVE as a supporting chapter. Owned around it: 2502.08606 (LLM-scale
    k-sweep) and 2503.16870 (truncated gradients are biased; sampling
    beats truncating at matched storage), but the entropy-conditional rule,
    the tiny from-scratch regime and seed CIs are unclaimed. Demoable
    twice — rerun our own pipeline at the recommended k and show batches/
    shrink ~50× with students unchanged, plus a shippable
    how_much_to_store.py. Race risk ~zero, because boring is the point.
    See F-52a for the experiment and its cost.


METHOD / ARCHITECTURE FAMILY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

26. DISTIL GEMMA INTO A JEPA STUDENT               [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Replace the autoregressive student with a JEPA-style latent-prediction
    architecture.

    SCOOPED and premise-conflicting. "KD via JEPA latent prediction" is
    owned at title level (JEP-KD 2403.18843) on top of the feature-KD genre
    (FitNets, TinyBERT), and JEPA×language is owned by the LeCun orbit
    itself (LLM-JEPA 2509.14252, VL-JEPA 2512.10942, JEPA-Reasoner
    2512.19171). Deeper: a JEPA student has NO softmax head, which deletes
    the reduced-vocabulary and logit-KL axis the thesis is built on, and
    turns the JSON task into the classifier the thesis exists to avoid.

27. DISTIL VIA SPECULATIVE DECODING                [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    The student drafts its own training data and the teacher verifies or
    replaces tokens, so the data lies on the student's own distribution.

    SCOOPED at title level by SKD (2410.11325, Google, ICLR 2025) —
    student drafts, teacher top-K accept/replace, interleaved sequence
    becomes the training data — with GKD, MiniLLM, ImitKD, DistillSpec
    (2310.08461) and SpecKD (2510.24021) around it. Also wrong for our
    pipeline: it puts the teacher back in the training loop, destroying
    the offline stored-distribution asset, and its motivation (exposure
    bias in long free-form generation) nearly vanishes for short
    deterministic JSON.

29. ANSWER-SLOT AUXILIARY SUPERVISION              [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Reserve part of the output or hidden state to hold the fixed-length
    final answer during training, with an auxiliary loss on it.

    SCOOPED as a family: answer-head-beside-reasoning-head is Dual-Head KD
    (2509.21487, already #23), auxiliary future prediction is MTP
    (2404.19737), latent-target auxiliary losses are Hierarchical Latent
    Prediction (2608.05806) and Coconut (2412.06769), deep supervision is
    2015, and Allen-Zhu's Physics 2.1 owns the phenomenon. Sliver: the
    intervention at the tiny floor as one audit row, where MTP's
    small-scale conflict predicts a clean negative.

30. NOISE REGULARIZATION FOR TINY DISTILLS         [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Add Gaussian noise to attention patterns or embeddings so the student
    sees more examples before it overfits.

    WOUNDED, and the mechanism kill is stronger than the citations. Every
    injection point is owned — embeddings by NEFTune (2310.05914),
    attention by Attention-Drop (2504.12088), noise-in-KD by SMART
    (2310.01209) — but the real problem is that NEFTune works by stopping
    overfitting so the model falls back on its PRETRAINED knowledge, and a
    from-scratch 3.4M student has no prior to fall back on. Predicted null.
    Two points in its favour from our own logs are recorded in F-30a.

39. MULTI-TOKEN PREDICTION UNDER KD                [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Ask whether distillation moves the crossover below which multi-token
    prediction hurts rather than helps.

    ALIVE — the best architecture-flavoured candidate on this ledger. Meta
    2404.19737 documents a clean crossover (MTP hurts below ~1.3B under
    hard labels) and all MTP+distillation work is about speeding up large
    pretrained models (2602.06019, 2603.23911, KAT-V1 2507.08297). Nobody
    has trained a from-scratch sub-100M model with MTP under teacher-KL,
    and the accepted failure story is signal sparsity of one-hot future
    labels — which we can replace with the teacher's full distribution at
    t+1 and t+2, already on disk. Either sign is a result; implementation
    is one extra head and one shifted KL term.

42. MUON AT TINY SCALE UNDER KL                    [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Test the Muon optimizer on tiny distilled students.

    ADEQUATE-to-ALIVE, but only with pre-registered non-obvious
    hypotheses: seed-variance contraction, a rare-class F1 interaction
    (2509.26030 shows Muon wins on tail associative memory, and KD puts
    mass exactly on rare tokens), and a temperature interaction. 2601.09865
    states verbatim that Muon × logit-distillation is underexplored. As a
    plain speed benchmark it degrades to weak; AdamW must get an equal LR
    sweep.

44. nGPT AS A KD STUDENT                           [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Use the hypersphere-normalized transformer as the student architecture.

    ADEQUATE. The mechanism is genuinely KD-specific and unclaimed: nGPT
    (2410.01131) has bounded logits, so matching a near-one-hot teacher
    demands a logit gap around 11, which forces the learned scale up and
    saturates every other cosine — and that scale is formally confounded
    with the distillation temperature. But nGPT's baseline gains are
    contested, the implementation is invasive, and the null-result risk is
    the highest on the board.

64. CURRICULUM / PRE-WIRED NEURONS                 [checked 2026-09-05]
    Train on maths, then continue with fixed input and output neurons to
    see whether addition is learned faster; or pre-wire a fixed-size
    representation for all maths problems and reuse the domain knowledge.

    DEAD — scooped and off-axis. GrokTransfer (2504.13292) is the first
    half verbatim (weak-model embedding transfer on modular arithmetic and
    sparse parity, ~5× speedup, XOR in one gradient step versus 80+), and
    its embeddings are not even frozen, which answers the "fixed neurons"
    detail against us. Abacus Embeddings (2405.17399, NeurIPS 2024) is the
    second half. Both are architecture results on synthetic algorithmic
    tasks, off the distillation axis entirely.

68. BINARY OUTPUT HEAD                             [checked 2026-09-06]
    Represent tokens as bit strings so the output head needs only two
    logits, and distil dark knowledge into the bits — motivated by
    vocabulary being a large share of a sub-100M model.

    METHOD DEAD: the output head is 0.75% of our parameters, so binarizing
    it saves 0.74% of the model (F-68a) — the motivation is right about
    vocabulary in general but points at the wrong half. The mechanism is
    also hierarchical softmax, Morin & Bengio 2005 and word2vec
    (1301.3781, 1310.4546). ONE SHARP FINDING SURVIVES, in F-68b: an
    independent-bits head saves parameters precisely because it cannot
    represent correlations, and the dark knowledge destroyed equals exactly
    the total correlation between bits — savings and loss are the same
    quantity, and a targeted search returned explicit non-coverage in the
    KD literature.

69. DOMAIN-SPECIFIC RETOKENIZATION                 [checked 2026-09-06,
                                                    TRIPLE-CHECKED]
    Build a domain-perfect tokenizer that merges terms like "toxicity"
    into single tokens, remap the teacher's distribution onto the new
    sub-tokens programmatically, and cut input and output vocabulary ~90%.

    METHOD DEAD — owned three times over by independent lines: Fast
    Vocabulary Transfer (EMNLP 2022 industry; follow-up 2402.09977) does
    domain tokenizer + vocabulary transfer + KD on vertical domains; KD of
    Russian LMs with Reduction of Vocabulary (2205.02340, already our #4)
    states that KL-based KD cannot be applied directly under vocabulary
    mismatch and introduces the alignment techniques, at 17-49×
    compression; Efficient Vocabulary Reduction for Small Language Models
    (COLING 2025 industry) is vocabulary pruning plus KD for small models;
    AdaptiVocab (2503.19693, COLM 2025) is the domain n-gram vocabulary.
    Cross-tokenizer KD is itself a crowded subfield (2502.11104,
    2603.22056, 2604.07466, 2503.20083, 2605.21699). TWO FRAMING ERRORS
    worth remembering: "redistribute programmatically" is the open problem
    rather than bookkeeping, and "can still output everything the teacher
    could" is false without byte escapes. What survives is not the method
    but F-69a — granularity as an entropy dial.


SAFETY / LIFECYCLE / EXTRACTION FAMILY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

20. REDUCED VOCAB AS A HARD SAFETY GUARANTEE       [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Claim that a student physically cannot emit harmful output because the
    harmful tokens are not in its vocabulary.

    DEAD. It is a manual token filter with extra steps, bypassable by
    coded language exactly as humans bypass word filters. "Cannot say
    slurs because the vocabulary excludes them" is a regex, not a thesis.

21. CONTINUAL OUTPUT-VOCAB EXPANSION UNDER DRIFT   [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Grow a deployed tiny expert's output vocabulary online as the data
    drifts, without forgetting what it already knows.

    SCOOPED at the mechanism level — Markov-Matrix zero-forgetting
    expansion (2605.04308), ASR continual output-vocab expansion
    (2401.04482) and the class-incremental distillation line all own it.
    Surviving sliver is the capacity-versus-forgetting frontier at sub-10M,
    where freeze-backbone zero-forgetting BREAKS because a tiny model must
    move shared weights to absorb new tokens — a real "why scale matters"
    reason, but a characterization rather than a method.

22. REVERSE-COMPILE A TINY NET INTO RULES          [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Extract explicit code or rules from a trained tiny student.

    WEAK FOR OUR DOMAINS. Sentiment extraction yields a trivial keyword
    classifier, which undercuts the generative premise the thesis rests
    on; maths extraction yields a broken heuristic, since the model gets
    arithmetic wrong about 22% of the time. It only pays off on a task with
    a clean hidden algorithm, which neither of ours is.

35. HUNGARIAN MATHS TUTOR                          [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Give the teacher Hungarian phrasing and a persona, internalize it, and

    COMPONENTS ALL OWNED and the combination is a substitution instance:
    prompt internalization is mature (Askell 2021, GenPI 2411.15927,

    Hungarian adaptation is active — Rácka (2601.01244, Jan 2026) swaps

    A FORK, not an extension.

59. TRANSFER TO A NON-LINGUISTIC LOGIC GAME        [checked 2026-08-31]
    Distil on a language domain, transfer the student to chess, and beat
    from-scratch initialization.

    BOTH HALVES OWNED and null predicted by degeneracy. Language-to-chess
    transfer is The Chess Transformer (2008.04057) from GPT-2 medium;
    "distilled init beats from-scratch init" is standard, mechanism owned
    by Abnar 2006.00555 (our #45). MECHANICAL KILL: 3.28M of our 3.4M
    parameters IS the input embedding, discarded the moment the vocabulary
    changes, leaving ~110K params of attention to transfer. WORTH
    REMEMBERING: a chess engine is a natural entropy laboratory (forced
    mate versus quiet position, with ground truth) if a
    verifiably-caused entropy dial is ever needed.

61. DIFFUSION × DISTILLATION                       [checked 2026-08-31]
    Four variants: an AR teacher distilled into a diffusion student,
    diffusion inside an LLM, diffusing an attention pattern, and diffusion
    for speculative decoding.

    ALL FOUR DEAD, four independent kills. Saturation: AR→diffusion is
    scooped by name (2606.06712), diffusion×speculative-decoding is
    carpet-bombed with 12+ papers, and diffusion-inside-an-LLM already has

    masked diffusion student needs bidirectional masked marginals, which a
    causal Gemma cannot produce at any data volume. Also contradicts

MEASUREMENT / PROGRAMME ENTRIES
------------------------------------------------------------------------

34. POST-GEN LOSS→METRIC DISSOCIATION              [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Each loss wins the metric nearest its own objective: annealing wins
    top-20, pure KL wins overlap and mean rank, pure CE tanks mean rank.

    SCOPED AS A PHENOMENON and near-tautological — CE supervises only the
    target token so tail ordering is unsupervised, KL supervises the full
    ranking, that tail structure IS dark knowledge (Hinton 2015), and the
    target/non-target split is the DKD decomposition. KEEP AS A THESIS
    FIGURE, not a research push: it ties the CE≈KL null to teacher entropy
    across three domains on one pipeline. Data corrections and the
    numbers are in F-34a.

37. ENTROPY-GATED DISTILLATION                     [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    The KD-versus-CE gap is a function of teacher entropy; measure the
    dose-response curve causally by re-tempering stored logits.

    HEADLINE DEAD, see #55 — scooped 2026-05-22 by 2605.23857. The margin
    that survives is that they key on STUDENT baseline entropy
    observationally while we would dial TEACHER entropy causally on stored
    logits, ranking preserved and no teacher retrained. Müller 1906.02629
    is the older causal manipulation but is confounded, since label
    smoothing reshapes logit geometry and needs a retrained teacher.

38. DIVERGENCE ZOO AT SUB-30M, OFFLINE             [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Does the reverse-KL advantage survive with no pretrained prior? With
    stored logits, every token-level f-divergence is computable offline
    and exactly.

    ALIVE — STRONG. MiniLLM (2306.08543), GKD (2306.13649), DistiLLM 1 and
    2 (2402.03898, 2503.07067) and f-distill (2307.15190) all use students
    ≥120M and pretrained, mostly needing on-policy sampling. The mechanism
    question has real content: mode-seeking assumes a capable student
    choosing among modes, and a from-scratch 14M student may need
    mode-covering first, which could flip the accepted ranking. A
    well-error-barred null is itself publishable.

40. KD-GROKKING PHASE DIAGRAM                      [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Do soft labels compress, eliminate, or transmit the memorization
    plateau? Distil from teacher checkpoints taken pre-grok, mid and
    post-grok.

    ALIVE — STRONG, with race risk. 2511.04760 (Nov 2025) showed KD from a
    grokked teacher accelerates grokking cross-distribution, but has no
    same-distribution analysis, no temperature sweep, no pre-versus-post
    teacher ablation and no seed counts; 2607.05104 has the many-seed
    statistics but hard labels only. Label smoothing is the essential
    control arm (2602.12039, 2501.04697). Needs a new tiny algorithmic
    domain and grokking hyperparameter archaeology first; the 2511.04760
    group is the obvious extender, 6-12 month window.

41. VARIANCE-CONTRACTION AUDIT                     [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Does KD shrink the seed lottery? Measure σ²_KD/σ²_CE per metric across
    alpha and temperature at 30-50 seeds.

    ALIVE — STRONG and the most feasible on the board, since it re-runs
    existing configs. 2305.17581 (NeurIPS 2023) PROVES KD partially reduces
    gradient variance, a theory prediction never tested at the
    outcome-distribution level, and 2510.12615 tests final-accuracy means
    only. Framing discipline is required: this is "testing a NeurIPS theory
    claim where only we can afford to", never "we ran more seeds". Natural
    methodology backbone for #37-40.

43. STRUCTURE-BEFORE-CONTENT ACQUISITION ORDER     [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    Split the training KL into structure-token and content-token
    components from stored logits, and test whether structure is acquired
    first.

    ALIVE. The acquisition-order literature is all hard-label pretraining
    (2309.07311, 2109.06096, 2205.11758), and our own 100%-valid-JSON at
    60%-task gap is the phenomenon. Hypothesis: teacher entropy ≈ 0 on
    structure tokens means KD = CE there, and all KD value localizes on
    content tokens. This is #37 at within-sequence granularity — the same
    law at a second scale. The frequency confound must be controlled.

53. THE COMPARISON BATTERY                         [checked ≤2026-08-26]
    "Does X help on tiny domain-specific distills?" run as a systematic
    battery rather than one cell at a time.

    ALIVE — TDK-worthy, with an active genre precedent: Narang 2102.11972
    (40+ modifications at T5-base) and, three months old, 2605.20798 (20
    modifications at 1.2B/3B with a seed noise floor and Bonferroni, only
    2/20 surviving). Four real differentiators: the distillation column
    does not exist in either installment, both stop at ≥220M, their noise
    floor uses n=3 seeds against our 30-50, and we have the entropy dial as
    an explanatory variable rather than a scoreboard. THE UMBRELLA
    PROPERTY: every plan item becomes a row, so no single null can sink it.
    Non-negotiables in F-53a.

56. *** THE SURVIVING CLAIM — HEADLINE ***         [checked 2026-08-31]
    "Distillation buys distributional fidelity and task accuracy at
    different rates — and on structured tasks, where the teacher has ~1
    effective choice, it buys neither."

    ALIVE and the strongest thing on this ledger. Untouched by 2605.23857
    (accuracy and PPL only), by Stanton 2106.05945 (fidelity ≠
    generalization, but never as a function of entropy or task type), and
    by the whole entropy-method family (#58). Already half-measured in our
    own logs, and it requires our instrument — in-eval distribution metrics
    plus full stored logits — so it is not casually reachable by a larger
    lab. Present it as effective branching factor exp(H), not nats: the
    teacher has one option here and three there.

66. THE COMPILATION / BENCHMARK PAPER              [checked 2026-09-05]
    "Best approach at low versus high entropy for a small from-scratch
    model" — a compilation paper that benchmarks the methods this ledger
    has been killing.

    ALIVE, and the strongest strategic reframe here. The genre exists
    (2504.14366, 2404.19319; surveys 2402.13116, 2407.01885, 2410.20011)
    and the closest precedent is the BabyLM Findings (2504.08165) with Baby
    Llama (2308.02019), but the entropy-indexed version does not, and
    entropy appears in KD only as a per-token weighting heuristic (EGAD
    2605.01732), never as a domain property to index method choice by. THE
    REFRAME: 60+ scooped entries stop being failures and become rows —
    you cannot benchmark methods that do not exist. The real blocker is
    that the entropy spread is n=2; both free fixes are in F-66a.

63. ANSWER-ONLY DISTILLATION OF A REASONING TEACHER

HOW TO WRITE AN ENTRY (read before appending)
========================================================================
Entries 1-19 of the original ledger were three lines; entries 55-69 had
grown to seventy. That happened because the file quietly took on a second
job. Keep the two apart:

THE LEDGER (this file) is a KILL RECORD. One entry =

    N. IDEA NAME IN CAPS                            [checked YYYY-MM-DD]
       2-4 sentences: what the idea was, in plain terms.

       2-4 sentences: verdict, who owns it, the decisive finding, arXiv
       IDs inline. Name the one surviving opening if there is one.

Nothing else. If an entry needs more room, what it needs room for is
almost never the idea — it is a FINDING, and findings go elsewhere.

A FINDING is anything that outlives the dead idea and would be cited in
the thesis on its own: our own measured numbers, a methodological trap, a
formula, a cost wall, a live claim. Those belong in findings.txt, keyed
F-<entry><letter>, cross-referenced from the entry in one clause.

EXCEPTIONS — entries allowed to run longer: the ones that are ALIVE
(#56, #66) and the ones carrying our own experimental data. Three or four
out of seventy, not the default.

PROVENANCE RULE: if a paper's body could not be read, say so in the entry,
as #55, #70 and the unread list do. Never let a search-engine summary pass
as a read paper.
