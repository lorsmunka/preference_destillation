F-70a  CROSS-FAMILY TEACHER ENTROPY IS CONFOUNDED BY THE TOKENIZER
       Per-token entropy is a function of tokenizer granularity: by the
       chain rule, merging tokens packs the same content into fewer steps
       and RAISES per-step entropy, splitting lowers it (see F-69a). So
       comparing "Gemma 4B entropy vs Qwen 4B entropy on the same domain"
       mixes model uncertainty with vocabulary granularity, and the two
       cannot be separated from the numbers.
       IT HITS US TWICE: our reduced ~525-token output vocabulary is built
       FROM THE TEACHER'S TOKENIZER, so two teacher families are not even
       scored over the same output space.
       ESCAPES: (a) stay inside one family — Gemma 1B/4B/12B/27B share a
       tokenizer; (b) normalize to bits-per-byte or sequence-level
       entropy, the standard cross-tokenizer-fair measure.
       APPLIES TO: any future teacher-swap experiment, #62 included.

F-70b  A PREDICTOR IS ONLY WORTH ITS VALIDATION COST
       Measuring teacher entropy is cheap — a few hundred examples of
       stored logits. Validating that it predicts student quality requires
       training students on EACH teacher, which means full data
       regeneration per teacher: #62's parked wall, days-to-weeks, out for
       November. GRACE (2511.02833) pays this differently — its score
       needs student GRADIENTS, so it instantiates the student and runs a
       backward pass. Ours would be cheaper to compute, but "cheaper and
       42 Spearman points worse" is not a paper.
       GENERAL FORM: for any "cheap pass predicts X" idea, price the
       VALIDATION, not the measurement. #6 died the same way.

F-69a  TOKENIZER GRANULARITY IS AN ENTROPY DIAL
       Merging tokens raises per-step teacher entropy; splitting lowers
       it; sequence entropy is conserved. Granularity is therefore a free,
       continuous control on teacher entropy with the DOMAIN AND TEACHER
       HELD FIXED — the fix for the n=2 entropy spread that blocks #66,
       with no second teacher and no data regeneration.
       COROLLARY: aggressive domain merging makes the model smaller AND
       the distillation harder, because it destroys the near-one-hot
       teacher that currently makes KL cheap. Compression is paid for in
       distillation difficulty at a measurable exchange rate.
       CHEAPEST TEST: build 3-4 tokenizers at different merge levels over
       the reddit corpus, measure teacher entropy per tokenizer on
       existing text. If entropy does not move, the line dies in an
       afternoon.

F-68a  PARAMETER BREAKDOWN OF THE 3.4M STUDENT  (measured 2026-09-06)
       Default config: hidden_dim 48, 3 layers, input vocab 68,328,
       output vocab 525.
           input embedding    3,279,744   96.00%
           transformer body     110,928    3.25%
           output head           25,725    0.75%
           TOTAL              3,416,397
       Under a 90% input-vocab cut: 464,637 total, an 86.4% reduction.
       CAUTION: "our model is 96% lookup table" is NOT a novel observation
       — the literature states plainly that embeddings are 45-62% of GPT-2
       and that the effect is pronounced at small scale. Ours is an
       extreme instance of a published fact. Use it as context, never as a
       claim.

F-68b  BINARIZING A HEAD COSTS EXACTLY THE TOTAL CORRELATION
       Hierarchical softmax with per-node parameters is exact but has V-1
       internal nodes, so it saves nothing. A single shared head
       predicting bits INDEPENDENTLY saves parameters precisely because it
       cannot represent correlations between bits — and the dark knowledge
       destroyed equals exactly the total correlation (multi-information)
       between the bits under the teacher's distribution. The savings and
       the loss are the same quantity.
       ENTROPY TIE-IN: a near-point mass factorizes almost perfectly, so
       binarization is nearly free where the teacher is confident and
       expensive where it is not.
       FREE TEST, ZERO TRAINING: fit the best product-of-Bernoullis to
       each stored 525-way teacher distribution under a code assignment,
       report the KL gap, sweep assignments (random / Huffman / semantic).

F-63a  A COLLAPSED CHANNEL CANNOT CARRY A PROCESS
       At reddit's teacher entropy the soft-label distribution is
       effectively the one-hot label, so logit-KL has already degenerated
       into CE. Answer-only distillation of a reasoning teacher therefore
       cannot transmit the reasoning, and the reason is measurable from
       stored logits rather than from a new training run.

F-30a  OUR STUDENTS DO OVERFIT, AND KD BARELY SMOOTHS
       Two points that argue FOR noise regularization (#30) even though
       the mechanism is predicted absent: (a) these students genuinely
       overfit in the test regime — task accuracy 62.7% -> 59.2% across
       epochs at 2048 examples; (b) measured teacher entropy is near
       one-hot, so KD contributes almost no smoothing of its own, which
       makes the usual "KD already regularizes" objection weak here.
       WHY IT STILL DIES: NEFTune-style noise works by stopping
       overfitting to instruction-data specifics so the model falls back
       on its PRETRAINED knowledge. A from-scratch 3.4M student has no
       prior to fall back on, so the engine behind the published number is
       structurally absent. Predicted null; a clean negative is
       defensible, but it is not a paper.

F-48a  DIVERSITY-SOURCE SHOOTOUT
       Rank diversity sources by complementarity per GPU-hour: checkpoints
       of one run (Snapshot Ensembles 1704.00109, free) vs seeds vs
       temperature vs distillation divergence (#38). The frame is owned by
       hyper-deep ensembles (Wenzel 2006.13570); the fresh sliver is the
       divergence column plus cost-normalized ranking under KD.
       Decision-relevant for #49 — it tells the pipeline what to cook with.
       SEED-RACING AFTER EPOCH 1 IS KILLED (by the researcher): learning
       curves cross, our own logs show accuracy decaying across epochs, and
       the saving is trivial at 3-minute runs. SALVAGE: "how often do seed
       curves cross under KD vs CE" is free inside #41's grids (Spearman of
       epoch-1 rank against final rank across 50 seeds) and quantifies
       whether ANY early-kill heuristic is safe in this regime.

F-49a  COMMITTEE EXECUTION REQUIREMENTS — WHERE IT WINS OR EMBARRASSES
       Selection split must not be the test split (leakage is the classic
       pipeline mistake). Report ORACLE coverage against REALIZED accuracy;
       the router eats the headroom and that gap is the central DES metric.
       Baselines: a dense 56M monolith at matched memory, always-run-all
       voting (routing vs voting), and best-single-of-10 (selection-luck
       control). Router labels are free on maths only.
       MATCHED-TPS REFRAME: at matched throughput the committee is "4x
       params held, 1x compute spent" — the MoE claim at model granularity
       — and #32's scaling laws predict it LOSES, which makes matched-TPS
       the sharper baseline and a coarse test of #32's sliver.
       CENTRAL THREAT: effective TPS ~ TPS_single / (1 + p_flag x hops),
       and p_flag ~ 1 - base accuracy. Maths at 21% accuracy flags almost
       everything, so rotation degenerates into always-run-all voting;
       cascade economics need a usually-right base (reddit ~60% is ok).
       MONEY PLOT: sweep the checker threshold to get an
       accuracy-vs-effective-TPS Pareto curve, with both baselines as
       points on it.

F-52a  THE LOGIT-STORAGE EXPERIMENT AND ITS COST
       Post-hoc truncate stored logits at k in {1, 4, 16, 64, 256, full} x
       seeds; reddit (prediction: k=1 is free at its entropy) plus post-gen
       (the actual curve); optional RS-KD arm at matched storage budget.
       COST: reddit is hours; post-gen is ~2 GPU-days at 3h per run —
       post-gen is NOT a 3-minute domain, and that asymmetry drives most
       scheduling decisions on this project.
       DEMOABLE TWICE: (a) self-demonstrating — rerun our own pipeline at
       the recommended k and show batches/ shrink ~50x with students
       unchanged at 30-seed CIs; (b) a shippable how_much_to_store.py that
       measures teacher entropy on 100 examples and returns a recommended k.

F-53a  BATTERY NON-NEGOTIABLES
       Uniform baseline recipe and a matched tuning budget per arm —
       fairness is where this genre dies. Effect sizes with CIs plus
       multiple-comparison correction (copy 2605.20798's protocol). And a
       why-layer (entropy, capacity) connecting the survivors: a scoreboard
       without explanation is a blog post.
       RACE NOTE: 2605.20798's authors live at 1-3B pretraining, so KD at
       the tiny floor is far from their lane.

F-62a  SECOND-TEACHER COST WALL
       Any second-teacher experiment needs full data regeneration: post-gen
       ran ~23 s/example and reddit is 15,627 batches, so days to weeks.
       Out of scope for the 2026-11 deadline; it is the follow-up paper.
       MITIGATION IF EVER RUN: keep the second teacher inside the Gemma
       family (3 1B, 12B or 27B) so the reduced-vocabulary machinery is
       untouched — and see F-70a, because leaving the family also breaks
       the entropy comparison.

F-66a  THE ENTROPY AXIS IS n=2, AND BOTH FIXES ARE FREE
       The benchmark direction (#66) is blocked because the entropy spread
       is not a curve: maths 1.004 and reddit 1.02 are the same point, and
       only post-gen at 2.88 differs. A two-point axis is not a benchmark
       axis.
       FREE FIX 1: per-field entropy from ALREADY-STORED reddit logits
       (safety / tone / toxicity / sentiment) gives four more points at
       zero compute. This is promising_lead1.txt Step 0, still never run,
       and it now gates two directions at once.
       FREE FIX 2: tokenizer granularity (F-69a) is a continuous dial that
       moves teacher entropy with domain and teacher held fixed. Build 3-4
       tokenizers at different merge levels, measure teacher entropy per
       tokenizer on existing text. No retraining, no data regeneration.

CODE FACTS THAT GATE THE PROGRAMME (verified from source 2026-08-31)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- TEACHER AND STUDENT TEMPERATURE ARE COUPLED in Trainer._compute_loss —
  both are divided by the same temperature variable — so the teacher-
  entropy dial DOES NOT EXIST YET. About 5 lines to decouple. This is the
  single change that gates the whole measurement programme (#37, #56).
- temperature ** 2 multiplies both loss terms, which is a gradient-scale
  confound whenever temperature is swept.
- train_single_example does zero_grad / backward / step PER EXAMPLE, so
  batch_size=32 is a file chunk, not a gradient batch. Effective batch = 1,
  ~61 ms/example, GPU idle. Real batching is likely 5-10x and is the
  difference between "we ran 5 seeds" and "we ran 50".
- Student parameter split at the default config: see F-68a.

F-V1   VIRÁG (long_context_recall) x GEMMA 4 12B: TEACHER IS A NEAR-ORACLE
       Generated 2026-09-17: 4,968 examples, 156 batches, 43 MB npz, 0 rejected by the
       596-token vocabulary, ~1 s/example (NF4, no thinking), mean 4.1 answer tokens.
       Teacher accuracy against the generator's ground truth: 4,962/4,968 = 99.88%.
       Every error is the same confusion: a "sport" question answered with an INSTRUMENT
       (6/483 on sport; 100% on the other nine attributes). Cause: the two templates share
       the verb "plays" ("plays tennis on weekends" vs "plays the trumpet"). Kept as is —
       it is the one explainable ambiguity in the domain, and the student inherits exactly it.
       WHY IT MATTERS: on this domain the teacher's errors are known and tiny, so a student's
       mistakes are its own retrieval failures, not inherited noise — the clean case for
       measuring what a 3-layer/1-head attention can find in a 200-300 token page.

F-E1   E-01: ANNEAL DIRECTION IS A NULL AT exp(H)~1 AND A STRICT ORDERING AT exp(H) 2.73
       Five kl_ratio arms (0.1->0.9, 0.9->0.1, flat 0.5, pure CE, pure KL). Reddit and
       math, 10 seeds each: no arm separates from any other by more than one seed std
       on task accuracy or overlap (noise floor +/-1.8 pp). Post-gen, 3 seeds: final
       overlap pure KL .305 > CE->KL .298 > flat .291 > KL->CE .284 > pure CE .264 with
       seed std 0.001-0.002; mean target rank 694 / 743 / 769 / 806 / 1071. Task
       accuracy there is structural validity and sits at 0.99-1.00 on all arms.
       WHY IT MATTERS: the same dial that does nothing on the near-deterministic domains
       orders the arms at ~7 seed std on the high-entropy one, in the direction "more KL
       late is better". That is the entropy-indexed claim in one table, and the reason
       every later experiment reports fidelity, not only accuracy, on post-gen.
