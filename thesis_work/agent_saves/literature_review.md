### Verified references (73)
#### A. Knowledge distillation: foundations
- [hinton2015distilling] Geoffrey Hinton, Oriol Vinyals, Jeff Dean (2015). Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network. NIPS 2014 Deep Learning Workshop (arXiv 2015). https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 -- The thesis loss (forward KL at temperature T, times T squared, mixed with CE) is this recipe applied per token over a reduced vocabulary.
- [gou2021survey] Jianping Gou, Baosheng Yu, Stephen John Maybank et al. (2021). Knowledge Distillation: A Survey. International Journal of Computer Vision 129, 1789-1819. https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.05525 -- Taxonomy for the background chapter: the thesis is offline, response-based (logit) distillation into an architecturally different student.
- [stanton2021does] Samuel Stanton, Pavel Izmailov, Polina Kirichenko et al. (2021). Does Knowledge Distillation Really Work?. NeurIPS 2021. https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.05945 -- Justifies logging fidelity (top-k agreement, KL, mean target rank) separately from task accuracy, and warns against reading 'closer to the teacher' as 'better student'.
- [menon2021statistical] Aditya Krishna Menon, Ankit Singh Rawat, Sashank J. Reddi et al. (2021). A statistical perspective on distillation. ICML 2021, PMLR 139:7632-7642 (arXiv title: 'Why distillation helps: a statistical perspective'). https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/menon21a.html -- Theory behind the entropy finding: when the teacher is almost one-hot (exp(H) near 1) its soft labels carry little beyond the hard label, so KD and CE should behave alike.
- [zhang2025capacity] Chen Zhang, Qiuchi Li, Dawei Song et al. (2025). Towards the Law of Capacity Gap in Distilling Language Models. ACL 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07052 -- A 4-12B Gemma teacher for a 1-100M student is far outside that ratio. Acknowledge the gap and, if affordable, add a smaller-teacher control (Gemma 1B shares the tokenizer).

#### B. Distilling autoregressive language models
- [kim2016sequence] Yoon Kim, Alexander M. Rush (2016). Sequence-Level Knowledge Distillation. EMNLP 2016. https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.07947 -- The 'teacher-generated' data mode is sequence-level KD; storing token logits on those sequences adds word-level KD on top, as Kim and Rush combined them.
- [xu2024survey] Xiaohan Xu, Ming Li, Chongyang Tao et al. (2024). A Survey on Knowledge Distillation of Large Language Models. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13116 -- Main overview reference for the LLM-KD section; places the thesis in white-box, divergence-based KD with teacher-generated data.
- [gu2024minillm] Yuxian Gu, Li Dong, Furu Wei et al. (2024). MiniLLM: On-Policy Distillation of Large Language Models. ICLR 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08543 -- The main argument for reverse KL. The thesis's divergence set includes reverse KL but applies it off-policy on stored teacher sequences, which is not what MiniLLM optimises; say so.
- [agarwal2024onpolicy] Rishabh Agarwal, Nino Vieillard, Yongchao Zhou et al. (2024). On-Policy Distillation of Language Models: Learning from Self-Generated Mistakes. ICLR 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13649 -- The standard fix for the teacher-forced vs autoregressive gap the framework measures. The thesis does not do on-policy KD (it needs a live teacher); a clear future-work item.
- [ko2024distillm] Jongwoo Ko, Sungnyun Kim, Tianyi Chen et al. (2024). DistiLLM: Towards Streamlined Distillation for Large Language Models. ICML 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03898 -- A divergence beyond KL/JS/TVD that would be cheap to add to the framework's divergence set, and an example of scheduling how much student data is used.
- [wu2025rethinking] Taiqiang Wu, Chaofan Tao, Jiahao Wang et al. (2025). Rethinking Kullback-Leibler Divergence in Knowledge Distillation for Large Language Models. COLING 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02657 -- Supports forward KL as the default for long multi-epoch training of small students, and frames forward-vs-reverse differences as a training-budget effect.
- [li2023curriculum] Zheng Li, Xiang Li, Lingfeng Yang et al. (2023). Curriculum Temperature for Knowledge Distillation. AAAI 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.16231 -- Closest prior art for changing the temperature during training; the thesis uses a fixed cosine schedule rather than a learned one and should cite CTKD when describing it.
- [clark2019bam] Kevin Clark, Minh-Thang Luong, Urvashi Khandelwal et al. (2019). BAM! Born-Again Multi-Task Networks for Natural Language Understanding. ACL 2019. https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.04829 -- Direct precedent for annealing the KD/CE ratio from KD-heavy to CE-heavy (0.9 to 0.1). Cite it; do not present the schedule as new.
- [anshumann2025sparse] Anshumann, Mohd Abbas Zaidi, Akhil Kedia et al. (2025). Sparse Logit Sampling: Accelerating Knowledge Distillation in LLMs. ACL 2025 (oral). https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16870 -- The thesis stores the teacher distribution restricted to a fixed reduced vocabulary, a different truncation; this paper gives the terms to discuss and measure the bias of dropping mass outside it.
- [wang2026bridge] Guanghui Wang, Kaiwen Lv Kacuila, Zhiyong Yang et al. (2026). The Bridge-Garden Dilemma in LLM Distillation: Why Mixing Hard and Soft Labels Works. ICML 2026 (accepted, per arXiv). https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26246 -- The closest recent theory for the thesis's KD/CE mix and its entropy finding: near-deterministic domains are almost all bridges, so the anneal direction should matter little there.
- [zhang2026egad] Hao Zhang, Zhibin Zhang, Guangxin Wu et al. (2026). EGAD: Entropy-Guided Adaptive Distillation for Token-Level Knowledge Transfer. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01732 -- Precedent for the framework's entropy-weighted per-token KL ratio. Claim the measured entropy-dependence across domains, not entropy-aware weighting itself.

#### K. Distillation frameworks and toolkits
- [yang2020textbrewer] Ziqing Yang, Yiming Cui, Zhipeng Chen et al. (2020). TextBrewer: An Open-Source Knowledge Distillation Toolkit for Natural Language Processing. ACL 2020 System Demonstrations. https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.12620 -- Annealed KD/hard-label weights and temperature schedules already exist in tooling; TextBrewer targets encoder models, not generative from-scratch students.
- [matsubara2021torchdistill] Yoshitomo Matsubara (2021). torchdistill: A Modular, Configuration-Driven Framework for Knowledge Distillation. RRPR 2021 (ICPR workshop), LNCS 12636, pages 24-44. https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.12913 -- Precedent for 'the configuration is the experiment'; the thesis uses typed Python run objects for the same goal.
- [vonwerra2020trl] Leandro von Werra, Younes Belkada, Lewis Tunstall et al. (2020). TRL: Transformers Reinforcement Learning. Software, GitHub (Hugging Face). https://github.com/huggingface/trl -- The standard off-the-shelf route for distilling a pretrained Hugging Face model; it needs a live teacher with a shared tokenizer and offers no vocabulary reduction.
- [goddard2024distillkit] Charles Goddard, Lucas Atkins (2024). DistillKit: Flexible Knowledge Distillation for Large Language Models. Software, GitHub (Arcee AI). https://github.com/arcee-ai/DistillKit -- The closest tool for stored teacher logits; aimed at post-training pretrained models at scale.
- [wang2025easydistill] Chengyu Wang, Junbing Yan, Wenrui Cai et al. (2025). EasyDistill: A Comprehensive Toolkit for Effective Knowledge Distillation of Large Language Models. EMNLP 2025 System Demonstrations, pages 787-795. https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20888 -- Shows stored (top-k) teacher logits plus teacher data synthesis in an open toolkit; its examples use pretrained Qwen students.
- [zhang2026kdflow] Songming Zhang, Xue Zhang, Tong Zhang et al. (2026). KDFlow: A User-Friendly and Efficient Knowledge Distillation Framework for Large Language Models. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01875 -- State of KD tooling in 2026 (throughput and scale); orthogonal to the thesis's role as a research instrument.
