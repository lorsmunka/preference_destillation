E-01 ANNEALING DIRECTION x ENTROPY            [DONE 2026-09-20 · all three domains]
     Does it matter whether the loss anneals CE->KL or KL->CE, and does
     the answer depend on domain entropy? Idea #3 called direction
     trivial; nothing in the literature indexes it by entropy, so this is
     the cheapest unowned cell we have.

     Five arms on kl_ratio_start->_end (0.1->0.9, 0.9->0.1, flat 0.5, pure
     CE, pure KL), reddit and math at 10 seeds each, 100 runs, ~3.3 min
     per run. RESULT: a clean null on both domains. Reddit task accuracy
     (final) 59.8-62.0% across arms with seed std 1.7-2.5 pp; math
     21.8-23.6% with std 0.9-2.2 pp; distribution overlap identical within
     0.7 pp on both. No arm separates from any other by more than one seed
     standard deviation, on accuracy or on fidelity — exactly what exp(H)
     of 1.02 and 1.003 predicted. The measured noise floor (+/-1.8 pp
     reddit, +/-1.7 pp math at n=10) is the number every later row is
     judged against. POST-GEN (exp(H) 2.73, 3 seeds, 15 runs, 2026-09-17
     -> 09-20): task accuracy (structural validity) is 0.99-1.00 on every
     arm and says nothing; the fidelity metrics separate cleanly and
     monotonically. Final overlap: pure KL .305 > CE->KL .298 > flat .291
     > KL->CE .284 > pure CE .264, seed std 0.001-0.002; mean target rank
     694 / 743 / 769 / 806 / 1071 (std 6-15). Direction matters here:
     CE->KL beats KL->CE by 1.4 pp overlap and 62 rank at ~7 seed std,
     and every step toward more KL late helps. So the entropy split is
     real: on exp(H)~1 domains the arms are indistinguishable, on the
     2.73 domain the ordering is strict — the row the study is built on
     (F-E1). Results: experiments/results/e01_anneal_direction.csv.
     Script: experiments/anneal_direction.py.

E-02 MULTI-TOKEN PREDICTION UNDER KD                      [PLANNED]
     Meta (2404.19737) found MTP hurts below ~1.3B under hard labels, and
     blamed sparse one-hot future targets. We have the teacher's full
     distribution at t+1 and t+2 on disk: does a soft future target move
     that crossover down to 3M from scratch?

     Needs mtp_heads / mtp_weight on TrainingRun, one extra head per
     horizon and one shifted divergence term — not built yet. Post-gen
     only; classification outputs are too short. Arms: 0/1/2 heads x
     hard vs soft future target. Confidence-gated emission at inference
     is scooped for large models (ConfAdapt 2602.06019, P-MTP): demo
     feature, not a claim.

E-03 MUON vs ADAMW UNDER KD                               [PLANNED, ROW ONLY]
     Does the optimizer interact with distillation at the tiny floor —
     seed spread, rare-token accuracy, temperature? 2601.09865 (Jan 2026)
     already runs Muon + KL distillation and wins 5/8 benchmarks, but on
     a pretrained Llama 3.2 3B with LoRA, single runs, no seeds.

     Needs an optimizer field on TrainingRun and ~50 lines of Muon in the
     trainer, then one experiment file. AdamW gets an equal learning-rate
     sweep or the comparison is void. From-scratch, full-parameter, 10
     seeds, crossed with teacher temperature — a battery row, not a
     headline.
