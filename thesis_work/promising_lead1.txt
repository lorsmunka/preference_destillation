THE CLAIM (one sentence, the whole paper)
-----------------------------------------
Distillation buys DISTRIBUTIONAL FIDELITY and TASK ACCURACY at different
rates — and on structured tasks, where the teacher has ~1 effective
choice, it buys neither.

Supporting frame: report EFFECTIVE BRANCHING FACTOR exp(H), not nats.
  math    exp(0.0043) = 1.004 effective choices
  reddit  exp(0.021)  = 1.02
  post-gen exp(1.059) = 2.88
"The teacher has one option here and three there." That sentence is the
thesis, it is legible to a TDK jury, and it makes the structured-domain
null obvious instead of disappointing.

WHY THIS SURVIVES (the margin, after the 2605.23857 kill)
---------------------------------------------------------
2605.23857 (Strong Teacher Not Needed, May 2026) already owns "KD benefit
concentrates where uncertainty is high" and "KD is not label smoothing."
CITE BOTH. Do not claim either. What it does NOT do:

  (a) it keys on STUDENT baseline entropy, observationally.
      We manipulate TEACHER entropy CAUSALLY, by re-tempering stored
      logits — ranking preserved, only entropy dialed, no retrained
      teacher, no confound. Muller 1906.02629 is the only prior causal
      entropy manipulation and it is confounded (label smoothing
      retrains the teacher and reshapes logit geometry).
  (b) Llama3 0.7B-8B, all PRETRAINED. We are 3.4M from scratch.
  (c) free-form pretraining only. The STRUCTURED vs FREE-FORM contrast
      is untouched by anyone.
  (d) it measures PPL / MMLU / GSM8K. NO distributional-fidelity metrics.
      => the dissociation is INVISIBLE to them. This is the lead finding.

Already half-measured in our own logs (postgen_kl_anneal_topk.csv):
  pure KL t5to3 : top20 67.19  overlap 37.67  mean rank 418.6
  anneal (n=2)  : top20 69.08 / 68.80  overlap 34.61  rank 486.3
  pure CE       : top20 67.55  overlap 26.69  rank 1068.9
Pure KL wins BOTH distribution metrics and LOSES top-20. That split is
the paper.


EXECUTION ORDER
---------------
STEP 0 — FREE, DO FIRST (no training, ~20 min)
  Per-field teacher entropy from stored reddit logits at the four value
  positions, plotted against measured per-field task accuracy:
      safety 74%  tone 63%  toxicity 59%  sentiment 43%
  Prediction: accuracy tracks per-field entropy (safety near-deterministic,
  sentiment genuinely ambiguous). MUST chance-correct for class imbalance
  (safety is skewed, which inflates it).
  This is the cheapest possible test of the whole claim. If the four
  points do not line up, the lead is in trouble and we learn it for free.

STEP 1 — THE GATING CODE CHANGE (~5 lines)
  Trainer._compute_loss currently divides BOTH student and teacher logits
  by the same `temperature`. Add a separate teacher-only entropy
  temperature. Without this the entropy dial does not exist.
  TRAP: the loss is multiplied by temperature**2. If the dial changes
  effective gradient scale, part of any "effect" is a learning-rate
  effect. Handle explicitly and state it — this is where a reviewer kills
  the paper.

STEP 2 — GO/NO-GO (2 hours, 5 seeds)
  Sweep teacher entropy on reddit. Reddit is CEILING-SATURATED (verified
  in ideaD-data01024-s1 epoch 0: topk 0.9998, mean rank 1.05, validity
  1.0, termination 1.0). Only teacher_student_overlap (0.32) and
  student_entropy (0.17) are live. If those two do not MOVE when the dial
  turns, the cheap-domain instrument fails and everything gets ~30x more
  expensive. Find out before committing.

STEP 3 — THE BATTERY (~30 GPU-hours on reddit, one weekend)
  ~10 rows x 3 arms x 10 seeds. Candidate rows:
    1  KL vs CE vs anneal ratio                    free, partly measured
    2  distillation temperature                    knob exists
    3  LABEL SMOOTHING CONTROL                     NOT OPTIONAL (below)
    4  divergence zoo: fwd-KL / rev-KL / JS / TVD  our unique capability
    5  top-k truncation k in {1,4,16,64,full}      free, post-hoc
    6  output vocab size V (aux% knob)             data exists
    7  noise regularization (embedding/attention)  predicted null w/ mechanism
    8  optimizer AdamW vs Muon                     ~50 lines
    9  depth vs width at matched params            free
    10 data scale                                  NEEDS matched-step re-run
                                                   (current sweep is confounded)
  Row 3 is load-bearing: if uniform soft targets help as much as TEACHER
  soft targets, there is no dark knowledge, only regularization, and the
  claim evaporates. Note 2510.15516 already published this result — keep
  the arm as a control, do not sell it as a finding.

STEP 4 — PRE-REGISTER, THEN SPEND POST-GEN
  Post-gen is ~3h/run, so the full battery is unaffordable there. BEFORE
  touching it, write down which rows should FLIP when exp(H) goes 1.02 ->
  2.88 and which should not. Then spend the ~12-15 affordable post-gen
  runs testing ONLY those predictions.
  This is cheaper AND stronger than running everything everywhere: it
  converts "we ran many ablations" into "we had a theory that predicted
  which ablations would flip."


NON-NEGOTIABLES (where this genre dies)
---------------------------------------
- UNIFORM RECIPE + MATCHED TUNING BUDGET PER ARM. If Muon gets an LR
  sweep and AdamW does not, the paper is worthless and a reviewer will
  say so in one line. Cut ROWS before cutting protocol. 10 rows done
  properly beats 20 done sloppily.
- SEED NOISE FLOOR FIRST, then effect sizes with CIs, then Holm/
  Bonferroni. Copy 2605.20798's protocol. Our 10-50 seeds vs the field's
  3 is the one honest resolution advantage — do not waste it by skipping
  the correction.
- Measured seed noise so far: task_best std ~0.8-1.1pp at n=5. Effects
  under ~2pp are not detectable at n=5; n=30 puts SE near 0.2pp.
- SELECTION SPLIT != TEST SPLIT anywhere a choice is made.


THE PRACTICAL PAYLOAD (the half that makes a journal care)
----------------------------------------------------------
Storage rule as a direct consequence of the law:
  batches/reddit_comment_sentiment = 90 GB of full 525-dim logits.
  At exp(H) = 1.02 the rule says store argmax only -> ~500 MB.
  ~180x, measured on our own pipeline, with students that do not get
  worse at 30-seed CIs.
Shippable tool: should_i_distill.py — point it at ~100 teacher examples,
it reports exp(H) and recommends whether soft labels will pay and what k
to store. One day of work. This is what an engineer actually wants and
nobody has published it.
